**THE VANGUARD**

[Game Name]  │  Class

*"**Where others hold the line, the Vanguard defines it. A master of reactive combat, the Vanguard draws strength from every blow that fails to land and turns enemy aggression into their own fuel. They do not merely absorb punishment. They punish the attempt.**"*

**Starting Equipment**

▪  Full Plate Armor (+4 Vigor Defense, +4 Agility Defense, Speed -5ft).

▪  Three Throwing Daggers (Piercing, Finesse, Light, Thrown 20ft).

If building from scratch: 120 Gold, and the proficiencies listed above.

**CLASS FEATURES**

| **Bulwark Focus  │  Level 1  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| You are at your most dangerous when enemies underestimate your defenses. You track a pool of combat points called Clarity. Your maximum Clarity capacity starts at 2 and scales to 5 at Level 10. You begin every combat with 0 Clarity. Clarity fades to 0 when combat ends or if you fall Incapacitated. You also gain Brace (Reaction │ Cost: 1 Vigor Point): as a Reaction when you take damage, spend 1 Vigor Point to roll your Vigor Die and reduce that damage by the result. You also gain Steadfast (Quick Action │ Cost: 1 Resolve Point): as a Quick Action, spend 1 Resolve Point to let yourself or an ally within 10ft Upgrade their next Save before the start of your next turn. |
| --- |

| **Generating Clarity** |
| --- |

| ▪  **Deflection: **Whenever an enemy attack against you results in a Glancing Blow, you instantly generate 1 Clarity. |
| --- |

| **Hold the Line  │  Level 1  │  Reaction  │  Cost: 1 Clarity** |
| --- |

| ***Trigger: ****An enemy attempts to move through your space or out of your melee reach.* |
| --- |

| As a Reaction, when an enemy attempts to move through your space or leave your melee reach, spend 1 Clarity to lock them in place. The enemy makes a Vigor Save against Difficulty 5. On a failure, their movement is halted immediately and they cannot move again until the start of their next turn. On a success, they complete their movement, but their Impact Die is Downgraded until the end of their next turn. This does not apply the Restrained condition: it is a positional denial that prevents movement specifically through or away from your space. It does not prevent attacks, spells, or other actions. Enemies using abilities that explicitly bypass opportunity attacks or movement denial are not affected by Hold the Line. |
| --- |

| **Article of War  │  Levels 1, 4, and 8  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| At Level 1, choose one Article of War from the list below. You may choose an additional Article at Level 4 and again at Level 8. Articles represent your specialization in battlefield control. Each Article provides a passive benefit and a Clarity-fueled active option. |
| --- |

| **Defensive Article: **Passive: You may wield a shield without the normal Speed penalty. (Cost: 2 Clarity, Reaction): When an enemy declares an attack against you, raise your shield and Downgrade the attacker's entire Impact Pool for that attack. The Downgrade also applies to their next attack against you this round. |
| --- |

| **Mobile Article: **Passive: Whenever you move at least 20ft on your turn, you generate 1 Clarity from the momentum of controlled advance. (Cost: 1 Clarity, Quick Action): Move up to 15ft without triggering Opportunity Attacks. |
| --- |

| **Aggressive Article: **Passive: When you make an Opportunity Attack, roll an additional Vigor Die and add it to the damage. Active (Cost: 2 Clarity, Reaction): When an enemy in melee reach attacks an ally, make an immediate melee attack against that enemy as part of this Reaction. |
| --- |

| **Guardian Article: **Passive: Whenever an enemy makes an Impact Roll against an ally within 10ft of you, you may spend 1 Clarity (no action required) to Downgrade that Impact Pool, as your presence draws the attack's force toward you. Active (Cost: 2 Clarity, Standard Action): Designate one ally. Until the start of your next turn, any attack targeting that ally must target you instead if you are within 5ft of them. |
| --- |

| **Coordinating Shout  │  Level 2  │  Quick Action  │  Cost: 1 Resolve Point** |
| --- |

| You relay critical tactical information in a single sharp call. Choose one ally within 60ft who can hear you. Roll your Resolve Die and add that result to their next Impact Roll this round. Additionally, that ally may immediately move up to 10ft as a Free Action in response to your direction. This movement does not trigger Opportunity Attacks. |
| --- |

| **Ability Die Increase  │  Levels 3, 5, 7, and 9  │  ****Passive** |
| --- |

| Permanently Upgrade one Attribute Die of your choice by one step on the Step-Die scale. Because your Defense Ratings are derived from your Attribute Dice, improving an Attribute Die simultaneously improves the corresponding Defense Rating baseline. |
| --- |

| **Threatening Presence  │  Level 4  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| Your physical presence alone dominates the battlefield geometry. Your melee reach extends by 5ft on Opportunity Attacks. Any enemy that begins their turn within your melee reach must use at least half their movement to leave your reach if they choose to move away: they cannot simply step out freely. Whenever an enemy within your melee reach targets an ally other than you with an attack, you generate 1 Clarity; this can happen at most once per turn. This restriction does not apply to enemies using abilities that explicitly allow movement without triggering Opportunity Attacks or that specifically bypass movement denial. |
| --- |

| **Multiattack  │  Level 5  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| You may attack twice instead of once whenever you take the Attack action on your turn. You may use the Combined Strike rule to pool both attacks into a single Impact Roll against the same target, subtracting their Defense Rating only once. |
| --- |

| **Vitality Surge  │  Levels 5 and 10  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| At Level 5 and again at Level 10, your maximum Hit Points increase by an additional amount equal to the maximum face value of your current Vigor Die, in addition to the standard level-up HP roll. |
| --- |

| **Iron Retribution  │  Level 7  │  Reaction  │  Cost: 2 Clarity** |
| --- |

| ***Trigger: ****An enemy deals damage to you.* |
| --- |

| As a Reaction, when an enemy damages you, spend 2 Clarity to immediately make one melee attack against them. This retributive strike is made as part of the Reaction and does not consume your Standard Action. The attack has its Impact Die Upgraded, as your focus sharpens under fire. |
| --- |

| **Fortress Stance  │  Level 8  │  Standard Action  │  Cost: 2 Vigor Points** |
| --- |

| You become an immovable point on the battlefield. Spend 2 Vigor to enter Fortress Stance. While active: your Base Speed becomes 0, you cannot be moved against your will by any means, and all Impact Rolls targeting you are Downgraded. You may exit Fortress Stance at the start of any of your turns as a Free Action. Fortress Stance ends automatically if you fall Incapacitated. |
| --- |

| *Note: Fortress Stance pairs with Iron Retribution and the Aggressive Article to make you the most dangerous stationary object on the battlefield. You bait attacks, Downgrade the incoming Impact, and answer a hit with a retributive strike, once per round, since you hold a single Reaction.* |
| --- |

| **Absolute Defense  │  Level 10  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| Your defensive mastery reaches its apex. Once per round, when you would take damage that would reduce you to 0 Hit Points, you may spend any amount of Clarity. For each point spent, roll your Vigor Die and reduce the incoming damage by the result, then apply the reduced damage normally. If the reduced damage no longer brings you to 0, you simply take it and remain at your new Hit Point total. If the damage would still reduce you to 0, you instead drop to 1 Hit Point and remain standing. This requires no Reaction and need not be declared in advance: you choose how much Clarity to spend at the moment the triggering damage would be applied. |
| --- |

| *Note: Inviolate Ward from the Warden Regiment and Absolute Defense interact independently. Absolute Defense protects you. Inviolate Ward transfers excess damage to you as True Damage. Both can trigger in the same moment.* |
| --- |

**VANGUARD REGIMENTS (SUBCLASSES)**

At Level 3, you define your approach to battlefield command. Your Regiment grants features at Levels 3, 6, and 9.

**REGIMENT OF THE BREACH**

*The Aggressor: You specialize in controlled aggression, breaking enemy formations while maintaining your defensive integrity.*

| **Advance  │  Level 3  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| You generate Clarity from offense as well as defense. The first time each turn you deal damage with a melee attack, you generate 1 Clarity. This is in addition to the standard Deflection generation. The Linebreaker does not wait to be struck: they press forward and build control through pressure. |
| --- |

| **Phalanx Formation  │  Level 6  │  Quick Action  │  Cost: 1 Clarity** |
| --- |

| You and one ally within 5ft form a coordinated front. Until the start of your next turn, any attack targeting either of you has its Impact Dice Downgraded if the attacker is not adjacent to both of you simultaneously. Enemies that want to hit effectively must split their positioning, which opens them to Opportunity Attacks. |
| --- |

| **Unstoppable Advance  │  Level 9  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| You cannot be slowed. You ignore the movement penalty from Difficult Terrain. Additionally, whenever you use Hold the Line, you may immediately move up to 10ft toward the halted enemy as a Free Action after their movement is stopped. You press every advantage. |
| --- |

**REGIMENT OF THE WARDEN**

*The Protector: You specialize in ally preservation, positioning yourself as an unbreakable shield between your allies and incoming harm.*

| **Warden's Mark  │  Level 3  │  Quick Action  │  Cost: 1 Clarity** |
| --- |

| Mark one ally within 30ft as your Ward as a Quick Action. While your Ward is marked, any attack that targets your Ward has its Impact Pool Downgraded if you are within 10ft of them. If the attack results in a Glancing Blow against your Ward due to this Downgrade, you generate 1 Clarity. You may only have one Ward at a time. Marking a new ally removes the mark from the previous one. The mark persists until you mark a new ally or until combat ends; it requires no upkeep. |
| --- |

| **Interpose  │  Level 6  │  Reaction  │  Cost: 1 Clarity** |
| --- |

| ***Trigger: ****An enemy declares an attack against your Ward.* |
| --- |

| As a Reaction, when an attack is declared against your Ward and you are within 5ft of your Ward, spend 1 Clarity to step into the attack's path. You become the target of the attack instead of your Ward. The attacker resolves the attack against your Defense Ratings, not your Ward's. |
| --- |

| **Inviolate Ward  │  Level 9  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| Your dedication to your Ward borders on supernatural. Your Ward cannot be reduced below 1 Hit Point by any single attack while you are conscious and within 10ft of them. Any excess damage from an attack that would kill your Ward is instead dealt to you as True Damage. This triggers automatically with no action required. |
| --- |

| *Note: Inviolate Ward does not prevent your Ward from being reduced to 0 HP by ongoing conditions, multiple separate attacks in the same round, or effects that explicitly bypass this protection. It applies to single-attack killing blows only.* |
| --- |

**REGIMENT OF THE TITAN**

*The Immovable: You specialize in absolute physical dominance, becoming a singular point of gravity that the entire battlefield orbits around.*

| **Gravitational Pull  │  Level 3  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| Your physical presence distorts enemy movement. Any enemy that starts their turn within 15ft of you must succeed on a Vigor Save against Difficulty 5 or spend 10ft of their movement moving toward you before they may take any other action. This is not a condition: it is a persistent environmental effect of your overwhelming physical authority. Enemies that are already adjacent to you are unaffected. |
| --- |

| **Iron Hold  │  Level 6  │  Standard Action  │  Cost: 2 Vigor Points** |
| --- |

| You reach out and seize an adjacent enemy with crushing force. Roll your Vigor Die against the target's Vigor Defense. On a hit, the target is Grappled. While Grappled, both you and the target have Speed 0. The target cannot make ranged attacks or cast spells that require movement. The Grappled creature may spend a Standard Action to attempt to break free with a Vigor Save against Difficulty 7. On a Glancing Blow, the target's Speed is halved until the end of their next turn but the Grapple does not apply. |
| --- |

| **Titan's Resolve  │  Level 9  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| Your willpower is as immovable as your body. You are immune to the Dazed and Blinded conditions. Additionally, you are completely immune to all forms of involuntary movement: nothing can move the Titan against their will. |
