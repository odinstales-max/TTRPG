**THE HARROWER**

[Game Name]  │  Class

*"**They walk at the edge of things, between the living and the dead, between sanity and the void, between the world as it is and the darkness that underlies it. The Harrower does not seek destruction. They seek understanding of entropy itself. The fact that this understanding tends to accelerate the entropy of everything nearby is, to them, an acceptable consequence.**"*

**CLASS CHASSIS**

| **Health Tier** | Fragile Caster: 5 + Max Vigor Die at Level 1. Roll your Vigor Die and add the result to your maximum HP at each level after 1st. |
| --- | --- |
| **Armor** | Light Armor, Arcane Vestments. |
| **Weapons** | Simple Weapons, Arcane Armaments (Wands, Staves, Rods). |
| **Starting Dice** | Focus d6, Resolve d6, Vigor d4, Agility d4. |
| **Daily Reserves** | +3 Focus Points and +1 Resolve Point per Level. |
| **Spellcasting Schools** | Entropy, Conjuration, Resonance. |

**Starting Equipment**

▪  A Void Staff (Force, Conduit, Heavy) or a Bone Wand (Focus Armament, Void damage).

▪  Funerary Vestments (Arcane Vestments variant: Focus Defense +1, Resolve Defense +2).

▪  A Harrowing Kit (Ritual components, binding rope, black candles, bone dust).

▪  A Scholar's Pack (Backpack, journals, ink, parchment, rations).

If building from scratch: 90 Gold and the proficiencies listed above.

**CLASS FEATURES**

| **The Entropy Pool  │  Level 1  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| You draw power from dissolution and decay. You track a pool of void energy points called Entropy. Your maximum Entropy capacity starts at 2 and scales to 5 at Level 10. You begin every combat with 0 Entropy. Entropy fades to 0 when combat ends or if you fall Incapacitated. From your first level the pool already has outlets. Creeping Rot (Free Action │ Cost: 1 Entropy): as a Free Action after you deal Void damage to a target this turn, spend 1 Entropy to advance that target's Decay by one additional stage. Grim Will (Quick Action │ Cost: 1 Resolve Point): as a Quick Action, spend 1 Resolve Point to Upgrade your next Focus Save or Concentration Save before the start of your next turn. |
| --- |

| **Generating Entropy** |
| --- |

| ▪  **Condition Harvest: **The first time each turn you inflict a negative condition (Decay, Bleeding, Dazed, Blinded, Sundered) on an enemy with one of your own attacks or spells, you generate 1 Entropy. Advancing an existing condition's stage does not count, and conditions applied by passive auras do not trigger this. |
| --- |

| **Void Lance  │  Level 1  │  Standard Action  │  Cost: 0 Focus Points** |
| --- |

| You hurl a concentrated mote of void energy at one target within 60ft. Roll your Focus Die against the target's Focus Defense. On a hit, deal Void damage equal to your Focus Die. At Level 5, Void Lance rolls two Focus Dice. As a zero-cost attack it is capped at two dice and scales no further: it is a reliable baseline, not a replacement for spending Focus on higher-Tier Entropy spells. Void Lance costs no Focus. |
| --- |

| **Death Sense  │  Level 1  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| You perceive the proximity of death and dying with instinctive accuracy. You always know the HP state of every creature within 60ft: full, wounded (below half HP), or critical (below one-quarter HP). Additionally, you know the exact location of any creature that has been reduced to 0 HP within the last hour within 1 mile, whether they are dead or merely Incapacitated. This sense cannot be blocked by any mundane means. |
| --- |

| **Life Tap  │  Level 2  │  Reaction  │  Cost: 2 Entropy** |
| --- |

| ***Trigger: ****An enemy within 30ft takes damage from any source, once per round.* |
| --- |

| As a Reaction, when an enemy within 30ft takes damage from any source, spend 2 Entropy to reach into the void energy released by their pain. That enemy takes additional Void damage equal to your Focus Die (reduced by their Focus Defense as normal) and you immediately regain Hit Points equal to your Focus Die. This healing does not apply against undead or constructs. |
| --- |

| **Entropic Amplification  │  Level 2  │  Free Action  │  Cost: 1 to 3 Entropy** |
| --- |

| ***Trigger: ****When you cast an Entropy school spell that costs 1 or more Focus.* |
| --- |

| You flood an Entropy spell with additional void energy as it forms. Spend up to 3 Entropy when you cast any Entropy school spell. Spending 1 Entropy adds one bonus Focus Die to the spell's Impact Pool. Each point spent beyond the first Upgrades that bonus die by one step instead of adding another die: 3 Entropy yields one Focus Die Upgraded twice. This follows the universal Bonus Impact Dice rule. Entropic Amplification cannot be applied to Void Lance, which costs no Focus. |
| --- |

| **Ability Die Increase  │  Levels 3, 5, 7, and 9  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| Permanently Upgrade one Attribute Die of your choice by one step on the Step-Die scale. Because your Defense Ratings are derived from your Attribute Dice, improving an Attribute Die simultaneously improves the corresponding Defense Rating baseline. |
| --- |

| **Wither Aura  │  Level 4  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| You radiate an aura of accelerated entropy that slowly corrodes everything near you. Any enemy that starts their turn within 10ft of you has their Decay automatically advance by one stage, or enters Decay Stage 1 if not already Decaying. Wither Aura alone cannot advance a target's Decay beyond Stage 2: deeper rot requires your spells and class features. This triggers once per enemy per round. This aura requires no action to maintain and is always active while you are conscious. Allies are unaffected. |
| --- |

| *Note: Wither Aura makes melee engagement with the Harrower increasingly costly the longer combat drags on. Enemies who close with you are rewarded with accelerating Decay.* |
| --- |

| **Void Form  │  Level 5  │  Standard Action  │  Cost: 3 Entropy** |
| --- |

| You partially dissolve into void energy for one minute. While in Void Form: you are Invisible, you cannot be Grappled or Restrained by physical means, your Void damage Impact Rolls are Upgraded, and you may pass through solid objects as part of movement (you cannot end your turn inside a solid object). Attacking or casting a spell suspends your Invisibility until the start of your next turn; the other benefits of Void Form continue uninterrupted. You cannot cast spells that cost more than 2 Focus while in Void Form. You may revert as a Free Action. |
| --- |

| **Vitality Surge  │  Levels 5 and 10  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| At Level 5 and again at Level 10, your maximum Hit Points increase by an additional amount equal to the maximum face value of your current Vigor Die, in addition to the standard level-up HP roll. |
| --- |

| **Soul Leech  │  Level 7  │  Standard Action  │  Cost: 2 Focus Points** |
| --- |

| As a Standard Action, cast Soul Leech as a Sustained spell on one target within 60ft. While you Sustain it (paying the standard 1 Focus at the start of each of your turns and making Concentration Saves as normal), at the start of each of the target's turns they take True Damage equal to your Focus Die (maximum 6) and you immediately regain that many Hit Points. This healing does not apply against undead or constructs. The drain is invisible and silent: the target knows something is wrong but cannot identify the source unless they succeed on a Difficulty 7 Focus Save. The spell ends if the target is reduced to 0 HP or if you fall Incapacitated. |
| --- |

| **Veil of the Dying  │  Level 8  │  Reaction  │  Cost: 2 Entropy** |
| --- |

| ***Trigger: ****A creature within 60ft is reduced to 0 Hit Points.* |
| --- |

| As a Reaction, when any creature within 60ft drops to 0 Hit Points, spend 2 Entropy to harvest the void energy released at the moment of their collapse. Choose one: you immediately regain Hit Points equal to 2 Focus Dice, or you immediately generate your maximum Entropy pool as the death feeds your connection to the void. You cannot use both effects from a single trigger. |
| --- |

| **The Void Ascendant  │  Level 10  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| You have crossed a threshold beyond which entropy no longer threatens you: you have become its master. You gain four permanent benefits. |
| --- |

| First, you are immune to the Decay condition. |
| --- |

| Second, Wither Aura's range extends to 20ft. |
| --- |

| Third, whenever a creature dies within 60ft of you, you generate 2 Entropy. |
| --- |

| Fourth, your Void Form no longer has a spell cost restriction: you may cast any spell while in Void Form. |
| --- |

**HARROWING PATHS (SUBCLASSES)**

At Level 3, you define your relationship with the void. Your Path grants features at Levels 3, 6, and 9.

**PATH OF THE GRAVE WARDEN**

*The Shepherd: You walk between life and death, guiding allies back from the edge and ensuring enemies stay down.*

| **Death's Reprieve  │  Level 3  │  Reaction  │  Cost: 2 Entropy** |
| --- |

| ***Trigger: ****An ally within 30ft is reduced to 0 Hit Points.* |
| --- |

| As a Reaction, when an ally drops to 0 Hit Points, spend 2 Entropy to intercede at the boundary of death. That ally is not reduced to 0 HP: instead they drop to 1 Hit Point. They remain conscious and may act on their next turn normally. This can only be used once per combat per ally. |
| --- |

| **Grave Mark  │  Level 6  │  Quick Action  │  Cost: 1 Entropy** |
| --- |

| You place a void mark on one enemy within 60ft. When the Grave Marked enemy is reduced to 0 HP, their death releases a pulse of void energy. All allies within 15ft of the target immediately regain Hit Points equal to your Focus Die. Additionally, you generate 2 Entropy from the release. The mark lasts until combat ends or the target dies. |
| --- |

| **Undying Purpose  │  Level 9  │  Passive  │  Once Per Combat** |
| --- |

| Your control over the death boundary becomes absolute. Once per combat, when you are reduced to 0 Hit Points, you instead drop to 1 Hit Point and immediately generate your maximum Entropy pool. For the remainder of combat, your Wither Aura doubles in intensity: enemies within range have their Decay advance by two stages instead of one at the start of each of their turns. |
| --- |

**PATH OF THE VOID CONJURER**

*The Riftcaller: You tear entities from the void itself, summoning creatures that should not exist into a world that cannot contain them.*

| **Rift Spawn  │  Level 3  │  Standard Action  │  Cost: 2 Focus Points** |
| --- |

| You tear a jagged rift in the veil between worlds and drag something through. A Void Spawn appears in an unoccupied space within 30ft. It has HP equal to 8 plus your Max Focus Die value. Its Vigor Defense and Agility Defense are each equal to half your Max Focus Die value. As a Quick Action, command it to move up to 20ft and make one melee attack, rolling your Focus Die against an adjacent target's Focus Defense for Void damage equal to your Focus Die. Any creature that takes damage from a Void Spawn has their Decay advanced by one stage. It is dismissed as a Free Action, at 0 HP, or when combat ends. Summoning a Void Spawn generates 1 Entropy. |
| --- |

| **Entropic Horde  │  Level 6  │  Standard Action  │  Cost: 3 Entropy** |
| --- |

| You tear three simultaneous rifts and pull through three Void Spawns at once. All three use standard Void Spawn stats. As a single Standard Action you may command all three simultaneously: each may move up to 20ft and make one melee attack, and they may target different enemies. Any creature that takes damage from any Void Spawn in this horde has their Decay advanced by one stage. |
| --- |

| **Apex Rift  │  Level 9  │  Standard Action  │  Cost: 5 Focus Points** |
| --- |

| You tear open a major rift and pull through an Apex Void Entity. It has HP equal to 25 plus your Max Focus Die value. Its Vigor Defense and Agility Defense are each equal to your Max Focus Die value. As a Quick Action, command it to move up to 40ft and make one melee attack rolling 3 Focus Dice against an adjacent target's Focus Defense for Void damage equal to the combined roll. Any creature hit by the Apex Entity immediately has their Decay advanced by two stages. The entity persists while you Sustain this spell (standard Sustained rules apply: 1 Focus at the start of each of your turns and Concentration Saves when you take damage) and generates 1 Entropy each time it deals damage. |
| --- |

**PATH OF THE PSYCHIC HARROWER**

*The Mind Breaker: You weaponize psychological trauma, using Resonance to amplify suffering and shatter the mental architecture of your enemies.*

| **Resonant Decay  │  Level 3  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| Your Entropy and Resonance spells reinforce each other with devastating synergy. Whenever you apply the Decay condition to a target, that target also takes Psychic damage equal to your Resolve Die with no roll required, as the physical corruption resonates into psychological trauma. Whenever you deal Psychic damage to a target, their Decay advances by one stage as the mental collapse accelerates physical deterioration. The Psychic damage dealt by Resonant Decay does not itself advance Decay, and Decay advancement does not count as applying the Decay condition: each half of this feature can trigger no more than once per spell or attack. |
| --- |

| **Trauma Echo  │  Level 6  │  Reaction  │  Cost: 2 Entropy** |
| --- |

| ***Trigger: ****An enemy within 60ft fails a Save of any kind.* |
| --- |

| As a Reaction, when an enemy within 60ft fails any Save, spend 2 Entropy to amplify the psychological impact of their failure. That enemy immediately takes Psychic damage equal to your Resolve Die and is Dazed until the end of their next turn. |
| --- |

| **Psychic Collapse  │  Level 9  │  Standard Action  │  Cost: 4 Entropy** |
| --- |

| You unleash a wave of combined void and psychic energy that assaults both body and mind simultaneously. Roll your Focus Die against the Resolve Defense of all enemies within 30ft. On a hit, each target takes Void damage equal to your Focus Die and Psychic damage equal to your Resolve Die, their Decay advances by one stage, and their next Impact Die is Downgraded. On a Glancing Blow, each target's Decay advances by one stage and their next Impact Die is Downgraded but no damage is dealt. |
