CLASS FEATURES


 The Living Conduit │ Level 1 │ Passive




 You are a channel for elemental and spiritual energy. You track a pool of spiritual power points called
 Animus. Your maximum Animus capacity starts at 2 and scales to 5 at Level 10. You begin every combat
 with 0 Animus. Animus fades to 0 when combat ends or if you fall Incapacitated. You also gain Ancestral
 Vigor (Quick Action │ Cost: 1 Vigor Point): as a Quick Action, spend 1 Vigor Point to roll your Vigor Die and
 regain that many Hit Points as ancestral spirits mend your body.




 Generating Animus




 ▪ Elemental Resonance: Whenever you deal elemental damage (Fire, Frost, Volt, Thunder, Acid) with any
 attack or spell, you generate 1 Animus.
Elemental Strike │ Level 1 │ Free Action │ Cost: 1 Animus




*Trigger: **When you deal damage with a melee or ranged weapon attack.*




You channel elemental energy through your weapon as you strike. Spend 1 Animus to infuse your attack
with one of the following elemental types: Fire, Frost, Volt, Thunder, or Acid. The attack deals additional
damage equal to your Focus Die of that elemental type. Choose the element each time you use this
feature. Each element carries a secondary effect on a hit.




▪ Fire: Applies the Burning condition: the target takes 2 Fire damage at the start of each of its turns for
two rounds, or until extinguished or magically healed.




▪ Frost: Halves the target's Speed until the end of their next turn.




▪ Volt: Downgrades the target's next Impact Die.




▪ Thunder: Pushes the target 10ft directly away.




▪ Acid: Sunders the target's Vigor Defense by 1 until the end of your next turn.




*Note: Elemental Strike's own bonus damage does not trigger Elemental Resonance. Elemental damage
from your spells and from other sources generates Animus as normal.*




Spirit Walk │ Level 1 │ Quick Action │ Cost: 1 Animus
You briefly step halfway into the spirit world, becoming insubstantial for a single heartbeat. Spend 1
Animus to move up to 20ft. This movement passes through occupied spaces and ignores terrain, walls,
and physical obstructions. You cannot end this movement inside a solid object. The movement does not
trigger Opportunity Attacks. You return fully to the physical world at the end of this movement.




Totemic Ward │ Level 2 │ Standard Action │ Cost: 1 Focus Point




You plant a carved totem in an unoccupied space within 10ft. The totem is a Conjured Object with HP
equal to 5 plus your Max Focus Die value. While the totem stands, all allies within 15ft of it Upgrade their
Focus Die for any Focus or Resolve-based Save. The totem lasts until destroyed, until you plant a new one
(which dismisses the previous), or until combat ends.




Healing Surge │ Level 2 │ Quick Action │ Cost: 1 Animus




You channel a rush of restorative spiritual energy into yourself or one adjacent ally. The target rolls their
Vigor Die and regains that many Hit Points. This requires no Healer's Kit. If used on yourself while you are
at or below half your maximum Hit Points, the Vigor Die rolled for healing is Upgraded.




Ability Die Increase │ Levels 3, 5, 7, and 9 │ Passive




Permanently Upgrade one Attribute Die of your choice by one step on the Step-Die scale. Because your
Defense Ratings are derived from your Attribute Dice, improving an Attribute Die simultaneously
improves the corresponding Defense Rating baseline.




Elemental Attunement │ Level 4 │ Passive




You deepen your bond with two specific elements. Choose two elements from the Elemental Strike list
when you gain this feature. This choice is permanent and cannot be changed after selection. For your two
chosen elements, Elemental Strike's secondary effect is enhanced.
▪ Fire: Burning deals 4 Fire damage per turn instead of 2.




▪ Frost: Speed reduction becomes Speed 0 until the end of their next turn.




▪ Volt: The Impact Die Downgrade affects the target's next two attacks rather than one.




▪ Thunder: Push increases to 20ft.




▪ Acid: The Vigor Defense Sunder becomes permanent for the remainder of combat rather than lasting
until the end of your next turn.




Multiattack │ Level 5 │ Passive




The elements surge through each blow. You may attack twice instead of once whenever you take the
Attack action on your turn. Both attacks may each trigger Elemental Strike independently. You may use
the Combined Strike rule to pool both attacks into a single Impact Roll against the same target,
subtracting their Defense Rating only once.




Wild Shift │ Level 5 │ Standard Action │ Cost: 2 Animus




You shed your physical form and adopt that of a powerful beast. Spend 2 Animus to transform. While in
Wild Shift, you retain your HP, all four Defense Ratings, and your Daily Reserves. You cannot cast spells in
Wild Shift. Wild Shift cannot be active simultaneously with Tempest Form: activating one ends the other.
You revert as a Free Action or automatically if you fall Incapacitated. Wild Shift lasts 10 minutes or until
dismissed. You may use Multiattack with your form's natural attacks.
Dire Wolf Form: Your Vigor Die is Upgraded, you gain a natural bite attack using Vigor that deals Slashing
damage, your Base Speed increases by 20ft, and if you move at least 20ft in a straight line before
attacking, the target must succeed on a Vigor Save against Difficulty 5 or be knocked Prone.




Cave Bear Form: Your Vigor Die is Upgraded, you gain a powerful claw attack using Vigor, your Vigor
Defense baseline is increased by rolling your Vigor Die and adding the result as a temporary bonus that
lasts for the duration of Wild Shift, and you may Grapple a target as part of a successful hit without
spending an additional action.




Elemental Serpent Form: Your Focus Die is Upgraded for all Elemental Strike effects, you gain a venomous
bite that deals Poison damage equal to your Focus Die on a hit plus Poison damage equal to your Focus
Die at the start of the target's next turn, and your Base Speed increases by 10ft.




Vitality Surge │ Levels 5 and 10 │ Passive




At Level 5 and again at Level 10, your maximum Hit Points increase by an additional amount equal to the
maximum face value of your current Vigor Die, in addition to the standard level-up HP roll.




Ancestral Guidance │ Level 7 │ Reaction │ Cost: 2 Animus




*Trigger: **An ally within 60ft makes an attack roll, a spell Impact Roll, or a Save.*




As a Reaction, when an ally within 60ft makes any die roll, spend 2 Animus to have the ancestral spirits
guide their hand. That ally may reroll the die and take the higher result. This applies to any single die in
the roll: an Impact Die, a Focus Die on a Save, or an Agility Die on an attack.




Elemental Fury │ Level 8 │ Standard Action │ Cost: 3 Animus
  You release the elements in a torrent. Choose one element and unleash it in a 20ft radius centered on
  yourself. Roll your Focus Die against the Agility Defense of all enemies within range. On a hit, deal
  elemental damage equal to 2 Focus Dice of your chosen type and apply that element's Elemental Strike
  secondary effect to each hit target. Elemental Resonance applies for each enemy hit.




  World-Speaker │ Level 10 │ Passive




  Your connection to the elemental and spirit worlds reaches its apex. You gain three permanent benefits.




  First, you may use Wild Shift as a Quick Action instead of a Standard Action.




  Second, Elemental Strike no longer costs Animus: it is applied freely to every melee attack.




  Third, once per round when you cast a Conjuration or Transmutation spell, you may immediately make
  one weapon attack as a Free Action. The elements and your body act as one.



SHAMAN****'****S PATHS (SUBCLASSES)
At Level 3, you define your relationship with the natural and spirit worlds. Your Path grants features at Levels
3, 6, and 9.

PATH OF THE SPIRIT CALLER
*The Summoner: You pull ancestral spirits and elemental entities from the other side, flooding the battlefield
with conjured allies.*


  Spirit Bond │ Level 3 │ Standard Action │ Cost: 1 Focus Point




  You tear a small rift in the veil and summon a Spirit Ally to fight alongside you. The Spirit Ally appears in
  an unoccupied space within 30ft. It has HP equal to 8 plus your Max Focus Die value. Its Vigor Defense
  and Agility Defense are each equal to half your Max Focus Die value. As a Quick Action, command it to
  move up to 20ft and make one attack, rolling your Focus Die against an adjacent target's Vigor Defense for
  elemental damage equal to your Focus Die, applying the element of your current Elemental Attunement.
  It is dismissed as a Free Action, at 0 HP, or when combat ends.
 Ancestral Chorus │ Level 6 │ Passive




 The spirit world echoes your summons. Whenever you summon a Spirit Ally, one additional Lesser Spirit
 automatically appears adjacent to it. The Lesser Spirit has HP equal to your Max Focus Die value, no
 Defense Ratings of its own, and deals damage equal to half your Max Focus Die value. The Lesser Spirit
 cannot be commanded independently: it attacks the same target as your Spirit Ally each turn
 automatically with no action required.




 Spirit Storm │ Level 9 │ Standard Action │ Cost: 4 Animus




 You tear open the veil fully and release a torrent of ancestral spirits across the battlefield. Place three
 Spirit Allies in unoccupied spaces within 60ft simultaneously. Each uses standard Spirit Ally stats. As a
 single Quick Action, you may command all three to move and attack, each targeting a different enemy.



PATH OF THE EARTHSHAKER
*The Terrain Master: You reshape the physical world to dominate the battlefield, controlling movement and
punishing enemies who cannot adapt.*


 Stone Grasp │ Level 3 │ Passive




 The earth responds to your combat instincts. Whenever you use Elemental Strike with the Thunder or
 Acid element, the ground around the target shifts and grabs. The target's Speed is additionally reduced by
 10ft until the end of their next turn, regardless of which Elemental Strike secondary effect also applies.




 Earthen Surge │ Level 6 │ Standard Action │ Cost: 2 Animus




 You slam the ground and send a wave of displaced earth surging outward. Every enemy within 20ft must
 make a Vigor Save against Difficulty 5. On a failure, they are knocked Prone and pushed 10ft directly away
 from you. On a success, their Speed is halved until the end of their next turn. Any conjured objects within
 the area take True Damage equal to 2 Focus Dice.
 Living Terrain │ Level 9 │ Standard Action │ Cost: 3 Animus




 You permanently alter a 30ft radius area of natural terrain around you. Choose one effect: raise walls of
 stone up to 10ft tall in a configuration of your choice, transform the ground into Difficult Terrain of
 churning earth, or sink the ground 5ft creating trenches that impose the Restrained condition on any
 enemy that enters them (Vigor Save Difficulty 7 to avoid). This reshaping is permanent and cannot be
 undone by mundane means.



PATH OF THE STORMCALLER
*The Lightning Rod: You call lightning, thunder, and wildfire down on your enemies, turning yourself into a
walking elemental disaster.*


 Storm Conduit │ Level 3 │ Passive




 Elemental energy cascades through you with unusual volatility. Whenever you use Elemental Strike, if the
 Impact Die triggers an Explosion, the Explosion automatically arcs to one additional enemy within 10ft of
 the primary target. That secondary target takes elemental damage equal to the exploded die result with
 no roll required. The arced damage carries the same element as the triggering strike.




 Chain Lightning │ Level 6 │ Standard Action │ Cost: 2 Animus




 You release a bolt of Volt energy that chains between enemies. Choose a primary target within 60ft. Roll
 your Focus Die against their Agility Defense. On a hit, deal Volt damage equal to your Focus Die. The
 lightning then automatically chains to one additional enemy within 15ft of the primary target, then chains
 once more to one enemy within 15ft of the secondary target. Each target in the chain takes Volt damage
 equal to your Focus Die with no additional roll. A creature can only be hit once per cast. On a Glancing
 Blow against the primary target, the chain does not activate.




 Tempest Form │ Level 9 │ Standard Action │ Cost: 3 Animus
 You transform into a living storm for one minute. Tempest Form cannot be active simultaneously with
 Wild Shift: activating one ends the other. While in Tempest Form: your Base Speed increases by 20ft;
 every melee attack you make automatically applies the Elemental Strike Volt secondary effect at no
 Animus cost; you emit a 10ft radius of crackling lightning that deals Volt damage equal to your Focus Die
 to any enemy that starts their turn adjacent to you, with no roll required; and you are immune to the
 Prone condition. Tempest Form does not allow spellcasting: you are the spell.




 *Note: Tempest Form stacks with Elemental Attunement if Volt is one of your chosen elements, applying
 the enhanced secondary effect (two Impact Dice Downgraded instead of one) to every melee hit.*
