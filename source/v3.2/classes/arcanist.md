**THE ARCANIST**

[Game Name]  │  Class

*"**Arcanists are scholars of the arcane, dedicated to understanding and manipulating the fundamental forces of magic. Unlike other casters who rely on instinct or divine favor, Arcanists meticulously control magical energies through formula and theory, pulling reality apart at the seams to weave it into weapons and walls.**"*

**CLASS CHASSIS**

| **Health Tier** | Fragile Caster: 5 + Max Vigor Die at Level 1. Roll your Vigor Die and add the result to your maximum HP at each level after 1st. |
| --- | --- |
| **Armor** | Arcane Vestments. No physical armor proficiency. |
| **Weapons** | Simple Weapons, Arcane Armaments (Wands, Orbs, Rods). |
| **Starting Dice** | Focus d6, Agility d6, Vigor d4, Resolve d4. |
| **Daily Reserves** | +3 Focus Points and +1 Agility Point per Level. |
| **Spellcasting Schools** | All eight schools. The Arcanist has the broadest magical access of any class. |

**Starting Equipment**

**•  **A Quarterstaff (Bludgeoning, Conduit) or a Dagger (Finesse, Piercing).

**•  **A Formulary and an Arcane Focus (Crystal or Orb).

**•  **Arcane Vestments (base Focus Defense +1, Resolve Defense +1).

**•  **A Scholar's Pack (Backpack, Book of Lore, Ink, Pen, Parchment, Rations).

If building from scratch: 150 Gold and the proficiencies listed above.

**CLASS FEATURES**

**Design Note: **No Defense Rating can be reduced below 0 by any number of Sunder applications. The Sunder floor is 0 for all four Defense Ratings across all classes and all sources.

| **Aetheric Dart  │  Level 1  │  Standard Action  │  Cost: 0 Focus Points** |
| --- |

| You fire a bolt of condensed magical energy at one target within 60ft. Roll your Focus Die against the target's Agility Defense. On a hit, deal Force damage equal to your Focus Die. At Level 5, Aetheric Dart rolls two Focus Dice. As a zero-cost attack it is capped at two dice and scales no further: it is a reliable baseline, not a replacement for spending Focus on higher-Tier spells. Because this costs no Focus, it does not generate Aether. Spell Weaving cannot be applied to Aetheric Dart: Weaves require a Focus expenditure to modify. If you are holding an Arcane Armament with the Conduit tag, Aetheric Dart inherits its Explosion Riders. |
| --- |

| *Note: This is your reliable baseline attack available at all times, even when your reserves are empty.* |
| --- |

| **Tactical Geometry  │  Level 1  │  Quick Action  │  Cost: None** |
| --- |

| Your mind constantly calculates angles and trajectories. As a Quick Action, choose one enemy you can see. Until the end of your turn, your spells and attacks ignore the Impact Die Downgrade penalty that enemy's Cover would normally impose. This does not negate Heavy Cover's effect on targeting; it only removes the Downgrade to your Impact Pool. |
| --- |

| **The Aether Conduit  │  Level 1  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| Your meticulous study allows you to siphon residual magic from the battlefield. Whenever any die in your Impact Pool rolls its maximum value during a spell's damage roll (triggering an Explosion), you instantly generate 1 Aether in addition to the Explosion's normal bonus damage. Aether is generated only in combat: you begin every combat with 0 Aether, and it fades to 0 at the end of combat or if you fall Unconscious. |
| --- |

| *Note: Aether is your tactical fuel. Upgrading your Focus Die via Ability Die Increases makes every die in your Impact Pool more likely to generate Aether.* |
| --- |

**The Aether Pool**

**•  Cap: **Your maximum Aether capacity starts at 2 and scales to 5 at 10th level.

**•  Starting Value: **You begin every combat with 0 Aether.

**•  Spending: **Aether is spent to fuel your Spell Weaving modifications.

**•  Decay: **All Aether fades to 0 when combat ends or if you fall Unconscious. The Aether Conduit also grants Phase Shift (Quick Action │ Cost: 1 Agility Point): as a Quick Action, spend 1 Agility Point to move up to half your Base Speed without provoking Opportunity Attacks, slipping through the gaps your wards open.

| **Spell Weaving  │  Level 1  │  Free Action  │  Cost: Varies (Aether)** |
| --- |

| ***Trigger: ****When you cast a spell that costs 1 or more Focus.* |
| --- |

| You alter the fundamental equations of a spell as it forms. By spending Aether, you apply a Weave to the spell. You may only apply one Weave per spell unless a feature explicitly states otherwise. Spell Weaving cannot be applied to Aetheric Dart. |
| --- |

| **Foundational Weaves** |
| --- |

| **•  Reach Weave (Cost: 1 Aether): **The range of the spell is doubled. If it is a touch or melee-range spell, it gains a range of 30ft. |
| --- |

| **•  Sunder Weave (Cost: 1 Aether): **If the spell deals damage, the target's Agility Defense is Sundered by 1 until the end of your next turn. Agility Defense cannot be reduced below 0. |
| --- |

| *Note: Weaves are applied after you declare the spell but before you roll.* |
| --- |

| **Arcane Resilience  │  Level 2  │  Reaction  │  Cost: 1 Aether** |
| --- |

| ***Trigger: ****When an enemy declares an attack against you.* |
| --- |

| As a Reaction, when an enemy declares an attack targeting you, spend 1 Aether to reflexively harden the magical field around you. The attacker's entire Impact Pool is Downgraded for that single attack. |
| --- |

| **Ability Die Increase  │  Levels 3, 5, 7, and 9  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| Permanently Upgrade one Attribute Die of your choice by one step on the Step-Die scale (for example, a d6 becomes a d8). Because your Defense Ratings are derived from your Attribute Dice, improving an Attribute Die simultaneously improves your corresponding Defense Rating. |
| --- |

| **Sense Magic  │  Level 4  │  Quick Action  │  Cost: 1 Focus Point** |
| --- |

| You attune your senses to the invisible flow of magic. Spend 1 Focus to become instantly aware of the exact location, approximate power level, and school of any magical effects, enchanted items, or spellcasters within 60ft of you for one minute. This sense passes through physical barriers and cannot be blocked by mundane concealment. |
| --- |

| **Cognitive Loop  │  Level 5  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| You learn as much from failure as from success. Whenever you roll a 1 on any Focus Die during combat (whether on a spell's Impact Roll, a Skill Check, or any other Focus Die roll) you instantly generate 1 Aether. This triggers even if the roll results in a Glancing Blow. |
| --- |

| **Aetheric Echo  │  Level 5  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| Your output expands to match your mastery. Once per turn, when you cast a spell that costs 1 or more Focus with a Standard Action, you may also cast Aetheric Dart at the same or a different target as a Free Action this turn. This is the Arcanist's Level 5 output expansion, equivalent to a martial class's Multiattack. |
| --- |

| **Vitality Surge  │  Levels 5 and 10  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| At Level 5, your Maximum HP increases by your current Max Vigor Die value. This occurs again at Level 10. |
| --- |

| **Dispersion Strike  │  Level 7  │  Reaction  │  Cost: 2 Focus Points** |
| --- |

| ***Trigger: ****When an enemy within 30ft begins casting a spell.* |
| --- |

| As a Reaction, when an enemy within 30ft begins casting a spell, spend 2 Focus Points to unravel the spell as it forms. Make an Impact Roll with your Focus Die against the caster's Focus Defense: roll your Focus Die and subtract their Focus Defense. If the result equals or exceeds the threshold set by the incoming spell's Tier, the spell collapses and is wasted: the caster loses that action and any Focus they spent on it. If the result falls short of the threshold, the spell resolves normally. Thresholds by Tier: Tier 0 or 1 collapses on a result of 2 or more, Tier 2 on 4 or more, and Tier 3 on 6 or more. If your Impact Roll is a Glancing Blow against the caster's Focus Defense, the spell is not countered but you immediately generate 1 Aether. (Thresholds tunable.) |
| --- |

| **Advanced Weaving  │  Level 8  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| Your understanding of arcane modification expands. You learn two powerful new Weaves in addition to the foundational ones. You may only apply one Weave per spell unless Master of the Weave or another feature explicitly permits otherwise. |
| --- |

| **Advanced Weaves** |
| --- |

| **•  Vicious Weave (Cost: 2 Aether): **Your spell gains the Vicious property. If any die in the Impact Pool Explodes, the bonus die is Upgraded one step on the Step-Die scale and can cascade further. This Upgrade applies once per Explosion die. |
| --- |

| **•  Impact Weave (Cost: 1 Aether): **Your spell strikes with physical force. If it deals damage, the target is pushed 10ft directly away from you. |
| --- |

| **Master of the Weave  │  Level 10  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| You are a savant of magical manipulation. You may apply up to two Weaves per spell. Additionally, once per turn, you may apply any single Weave that costs 1 Aether to your spell without spending any Aether; this free application occupies one of the spell's two Weave slots. Efficient Creation (Aegis Thesis) and Master of the Weave each satisfy a different Weave slot: at Level 10, an Aegis Arcanist may apply Reach Weave for free through Efficient Creation and one additional 1-Aether Weave for free through Master of the Weave on the same spell. |
| --- |

**Arcane Thesis (Subclasses)**

At 3rd Level, you commit to a specialized field of arcane theory that defines your approach to magic. Your Thesis grants features at Levels 3, 6, and 9.

**Thesis of the Aegis**

***The Architect: ****You specialize in structural magic, shaping force into defensive terrain and protective barriers for your allies.*

| **Aegis Weave  │  Level 3  │  Quick Action  │  Cost: 1 Aether** |
| --- |

| You weave a defensive barrier from raw magical force. As a Quick Action, spend 1 Aether to erect a 5ft pane of hard-light in an unoccupied space within 30ft. Any creature standing directly behind the pane benefits from Heavy Cover (all enemy Impact Dice targeting them are Downgraded). The pane remains until reduced to 0 Hit Points or until combat ends. The pane has HP equal to your Max Focus Die value. |
| --- |

| **Sanctuary Weave  │  Level 6  │  Free Action  │  Cost: 2 Aether** |
| --- |

| ***Trigger: ****When you cast a spell that costs 1 or more Focus, targeting yourself or an ally.* |
| --- |

| You learn a complex new defensive Weave. Apply this to any Focus-costing spell targeting yourself or one ally. The target projects a 10ft arcane aura until the start of your next turn. Any ally standing within this aura benefits from Light Cover (the attacker's Impact Die is Downgraded) against attacks originating from outside the aura. Sanctuary Weave counts as your one Weave application for that spell. |
| --- |

| **Efficient Creation  │  Level 9  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| Your defensive architecture flows from your hands as naturally as breathing. You may apply the Reach Weave to any Focus-costing spell without spending its Aether cost: the 1 Aether cost is waived. This free application counts as your one Weave for that spell. It applies once per spell cast and cannot be applied a second time on the same cast by any other rule unless that rule explicitly permits a second Weave. |
| --- |

**Thesis of Control**

***The Puppeteer: ****You focus on offensive lockdown, turning your magic into a snare that cripples enemy mobility.*

| **Aetheric Tether  │  Level 3  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| Your magic latches onto the physical forms of your targets. Whenever you generate 1 Aether from The Aether Conduit (that is, whenever a die in your Impact Pool Explodes on a spell) the specific enemy you were targeting becomes Tethered until the end of your next turn. A Tethered enemy's Speed is halved. Multiple Explosions on the same cast do not stack the duration. |
| --- |

| **Chain Weave  │  Level 6  │  Free Action  │  Cost: 2 Aether** |
| --- |

| ***Trigger: ****When you cast a single-target spell that costs 1 or more Focus.* |
| --- |

| You learn a volatile offensive Weave. If the modified spell successfully deals damage to the primary target, the magic violently arcs to a second target of your choice within 15ft of the primary. Roll your full Impact Pool a second time against the second target's applicable Defense Rating. The second arc can Explode normally. Chain Weave counts as your one Weave for that spell. |
| --- |

| **Puppet Master  │  Level 9  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| Your tethers wrap around the enemy's nervous system. Whenever you cast any spell that costs Focus and it deals damage to a Tethered enemy, that enemy automatically suffers the Dazed condition (the Impact Die for their next attack is Downgraded and they cannot take Reactions until the start of their next turn). Puppet Master's Daze applies once per spell cast against a Tethered target: it does not trigger multiple times from a single spell, even if it hits the Tethered enemy through multiple damage instances. This applies in addition to whatever other effect the spell produces. |
| --- |

**Thesis of the Transmuter**

***The Reshaper: ****You redirect arcane energy into physical transformation, warping matter and the battlefield itself.*

| **Material Weave  │  Level 3  │  Free Action  │  Cost: 1 Aether** |
| --- |

| ***Trigger: ****When you cast a spell that costs 1 or more Focus.* |
| --- |

| You learn a Transmutation-infused Weave that destabilizes the target's physical structure on contact. If the spell deals damage, the target's Vigor Defense is Sundered by 1 until the end of your next turn. Vigor Defense cannot be reduced below 0. Material Weave counts as your one Weave for that spell. |
| --- |

| **Transposition  │  Level 6  │  Quick Action  │  Cost: 2 Aether** |
| --- |

| You rearrange the physical positions of two willing allies or one ally and yourself within 30ft. Both creatures instantly exchange positions. Neither movement triggers Opportunity Attacks. Neither creature needs to be able to reach the other's position normally: the transposition overrides physical pathing. |
| --- |

| **Arcane Transmutation  │  Level 9  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| Your command of physical transformation becomes definitive. Whenever you Sunder a Defense Rating with any of your Weaves, that Sunder is permanent for the remainder of combat rather than expiring at the end of your next turn. Additionally, once per round, when you deal damage with a Focus-costing spell that targets a single creature, you may spend 2 Aether to have that spell deal its damage as True Damage: the transmutation energy is applied directly, unmitigated by the target's Defense. |
| --- |
