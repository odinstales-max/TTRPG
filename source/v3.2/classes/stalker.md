**THE STALKER**

[Game Name]  │  Class

*"**They move through the wastes like a ghost, leaving only confusion and bodies in their wake. The Stalker is a predator by design: patient, adaptable, and lethal. Where the Assassin strikes once with precision, the Stalker hunts over time, building pressure, reading the field, and choosing the perfect moment to collapse the trap they have been laying since the first breath of combat.**"*

**CLASS CHASSIS**

**CLASS FEATURES**

| **The Hunter's Instinct  │  Level 1  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| You are always reading the battlefield, cataloguing weaknesses and timing your strike for maximum effect. You track a pool of predatory focus points called Guile. Your maximum Guile capacity starts at 2 and scales to 5 at Level 10. You begin every combat with 0 Guile. Guile fades to 0 when combat ends or if you fall Incapacitated. From your first level Guile already has an outlet, and your reserves do too. Hunter's Strike (Free Action │ Cost: 1 Guile): as a Free Action when you hit your Marked Quarry, spend 1 Guile to add one Impact Die to that attack, following the Bonus Impact Dice rule. Read the Wind (Quick Action │ Cost: 1 Agility Point or 1 Focus Point): as a Quick Action, spend 1 Agility Point to move up to 15ft without provoking Opportunity Attacks, or spend 1 Focus Point to Upgrade your next Impact Die against a creature you have observed this combat. |
| --- |

| **Generating Guile** |
| --- |

| ▪  **Predator's Patience: **Whenever you hit an enemy that is unaware of your presence, in Cover, or suffering from a condition, you generate 1 Guile. |
| --- |

| **Marked Quarry  │  Level 1  │  Quick Action  │  Cost: None** |
| --- |

| Designate one creature you can see as your Quarry. You always know the Quarry's exact position as long as you are on the same plane of existence, regardless of concealment or distance. Your Impact Die on all attacks against your Quarry is Upgraded. You can only have one Quarry at a time. Marking a new Quarry removes the mark from the previous one. The mark persists until the Quarry is reduced to 0 Hit Points, until you mark a new target, or until combat ends. |
| --- |

| **Ambush Predator  │  Level 1  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| You excel at striking from unexpected angles. Whenever you attack a target that has not yet acted this combat, or a target that cannot see you, your Impact Die for that attack is Upgraded a second time. If Marked Quarry's Upgrade and Ambush Predator's Upgrade both apply, both stack: your Impact Die is Upgraded twice total. |
| --- |

| *Note: On the first round of combat before most enemies have acted, nearly every attack qualifies for Ambush Predator. Open aggressively.* |
| --- |

| **Hunting Formation  │  Level 2  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| You have learned to exploit the openings your allies create. Whenever you make an attack against an enemy that is also adjacent to at least one of your allies, your Impact Die is Upgraded. This stacks with other Upgrades from Marked Quarry and Ambush Predator, but a single Impact Roll can benefit from a maximum of two Upgrades from your Stalker class features, regardless of how many trigger. |
| --- |

| **Stim-Shot  │  Level 2  │  Quick Action  │  Cost: 1 Focus Point** |
| --- |

| You administer a fast-acting combat stimulant to yourself or an adjacent ally. The target immediately rolls their Vigor Die and regains that many Hit Points. This requires no Healer's Kit. You cannot use Stim-Shot on the same creature more than once per Short Rest. |
| --- |

| **Ability Die Increase  │  Levels 3, 5, 7, and 9  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| Permanently Upgrade one Attribute Die of your choice by one step on the Step-Die scale. Because your Defense Ratings are derived from your Attribute Dice, improving an Attribute Die simultaneously improves the corresponding Defense Rating baseline. |
| --- |

| **Sabotage  │  Level 4  │  Standard Action  │  Cost: 2 Guile** |
| --- |

| You identify and exploit a critical weakness in one target's defenses or equipment. Choose one of the following effects, applied to your Quarry or any enemy within 30ft. |
| --- |

| ▪  **Armor Breach: **The target's Vigor Defense is Sundered by 2 until the end of their next turn. |
| --- |

| ▪  **Weapon Tangle: **The target's Impact Die is Downgraded on their next attack. |
| --- |

| ▪  **Sensory Disruption: **Roll your Focus Die against the target's Agility Defense. On a hit, the target is Blinded until the end of their next turn. On a Glancing Blow, the target's next Impact Die is Downgraded. |
| --- |

| **Multiattack  │  Level 5  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| Your hunting instincts allow for rapid follow-up strikes. You may attack twice instead of once whenever you take the Attack action on your turn. You may use the Combined Strike rule to pool both attacks into a single Impact Roll against the same target, subtracting their Defense Rating only once. |
| --- |

| **Visceral Quarry  │  Level 5  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| You have studied your prey long enough to know precisely where its body fails. Once per round, one attack you make against your Marked Quarry may be declared Visceral: roll to connect as normal, and on a hit the full roll is dealt as True Damage instead of being reduced by the Defense Rating. This may be declared only on a single, distinct Impact Roll, never on a Combined Strike pooled roll; if you attack twice, Visceral applies to only one of your separate Impact Rolls. On a Glancing Blow the attack deals no damage and you gain 1 Momentum Token as normal. |
| --- |

| **Apex Predator  │  Level 5  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| Your Quarry cannot escape your attention or your reach. Whenever your Quarry attempts to move away from you, you may immediately move up to your Base Speed toward them as a Free Action. This movement does not trigger Opportunity Attacks. If your Quarry attempts to hide or become Invisible, you may spend 1 Guile to maintain awareness of their exact position for one round, negating their Hidden status against you. |
| --- |

| **Vitality Surge  │  Levels 5 and 10  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| At Level 5 and again at Level 10, your maximum Hit Points increase by an additional amount equal to the maximum face value of your current Vigor Die, in addition to the standard level-up HP roll. |
| --- |

| **Predator's Reflex  │  Level 7  │  Reaction  │  Cost: 1 Agility Point** |
| --- |

| ***Trigger: ****You are caught in an area-of-effect attack.* |
| --- |

| As a Reaction, when you are caught in an area-of-effect attack such as an explosion, wave, or zone spell, spend 1 Agility Point to read the geometry and slip through the gap. You negate all damage from the triggering effect and move up to 10ft in any direction. This movement does not trigger Opportunity Attacks. |
| --- |

| **Death From Above  │  Level 8  │  Standard Action  │  Cost: 3 Guile** |
| --- |

| You drop from an elevated position onto a target below with devastating force. You must be at least 10ft above your target: elevation can be gained through climbing, jumping, ledges, or any other means available in the current environment. Descend up to 30ft and land adjacent to the target. Roll your Agility Die against the target's Vigor Defense. On a hit, deal Piercing damage equal to 3 Agility Dice and the target is knocked Prone. The target is also Dazed until the end of their next turn from the shock of the impact. On a Glancing Blow, deal Piercing damage equal to your Agility Die and the target is knocked Prone only. |
| --- |

| *Note: Death From Above generates 1 Guile from Predator**'**s Patience if the target was unaware of your position before the attack.* |
| --- |

| **One With the Wastes  │  Level 10  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| You are a ghost in any environment. You gain three permanent benefits. |
| --- |

| First, you can move at full speed while Hidden without automatically revealing yourself: you only reveal your position when you attack or cast a spell. |
| --- |

| Second, your Marked Quarry designation persists across Short Rests. You do not lose your Quarry when combat ends unless you choose to release them. Guile still resets to 0 at the end of each combat even when Quarry persists. |
| --- |

| Third, whenever you reduce your Quarry to 0 Hit Points, you immediately generate your maximum Guile (5 points) and may designate a new Quarry as a Free Action. |
| --- |

**STALKER****'****S PARADIGMS (SUBCLASSES)**

At Level 3, you define your hunting methodology. Your Paradigm grants features at Levels 3, 6, and 9.

**PARADIGM OF THE GHOST HUNTER**

*The Phantom: You specialize in total concealment, striking from positions enemies cannot track and vanishing before they can retaliate.*

| **Shroud Step  │  Level 3  │  Quick Action  │  Cost: 1 Agility Point** |
| --- |

| Spend 1 Agility to immediately become Hidden regardless of your current environment. You do not need cover or darkness: Shroud Step is a trained technique of reading sightlines and vanishing into whatever visual noise the environment provides. The Hidden status is lost the moment you make an attack or cast a spell. |
| --- |

| **Phantom Strike  │  Level 6  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| Whenever you attack from Hidden status, your Impact Die is Upgraded and you remain Hidden after the attack: you do not reveal your position. The enemy knows they were struck but cannot pinpoint your location. However, if a Phantom Strike results in a Glancing Blow, your position is revealed and your Hidden status ends: a strike that fails to breach the target's Defense does not conceal its source. You may attack from Hidden this way a maximum of twice per round before your position becomes apparent. Enemies may still attempt to locate you as described in the Hidden condition. |
| --- |

| **Unseen Hand  │  Level 9  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| Your phantom nature becomes absolute. You are immune to the effects of magical detection and Divination spells of Tier 2 or lower: they cannot locate or identify you. Additionally, enemies that cannot see you have their Impact Dice Downgraded on attacks against you, even if they know your general location from sound or other senses. |
| --- |

**PARADIGM OF THE PACK-BOND**

*The Pack Leader: You amplify your allies through coordinated predation, turning every teammate into an extension of your own hunting instinct.*

| **Pack Leader  │  Level 3  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| Your allies are more dangerous near your Quarry. Whenever an ally attacks your Marked Quarry, their Impact Die is Upgraded. This Upgrade applies in addition to any other Upgrade the ally has from their own features. The Quarry becomes the shared focal point of the entire party's aggression. |
| --- |

| **Coordinated Strike  │  Level 6  │  Free Action  │  Cost: 2 Guile  │  Once Per Round** |
| --- |

| ***Trigger: ****When an ally hits your Marked Quarry.* |
| --- |

| As a Free Action, when an ally hits your Quarry, spend 2 Guile to immediately make one attack against that same Quarry as part of the same moment. This attack has its Impact Die Upgraded. It does not consume your Standard Action. Coordinated Strike can only trigger once per round regardless of how many times allies hit your Quarry. |
| --- |

| **Pack Frenzy  │  Level 9  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| Your pack's coordination reaches a fever pitch. Whenever three or more different creatures attack your Marked Quarry in the same round, the Quarry's Vigor Defense is Sundered by 1 for the remainder of combat. This Sunder stacks each round the condition is met and cannot reduce the Quarry's Vigor Defense below 0. |
| --- |

**PARADIGM OF THE TRAPPER**

*The Architect: You reshape the battlefield with caltrops, tripwires, and deployable hazards, turning the environment itself into your greatest weapon.*

| **Field Craft  │  Level 3  │  Quick Action  │  Cost: 1 Focus Point** |
| --- |

| You deploy one trap from your Stalker's Kit as a Quick Action. You may place a trap in any unoccupied space within 10ft. Traps remain until triggered or until combat ends. You may have a maximum of three active traps simultaneously. A fourth trap replaces the oldest one. |
| --- |

| **Trap Deployables** |
| --- |

| ▪  **Caltrops: **5ft radius. Any enemy that enters the area has their Speed reduced to 0 until they spend a Standard Action to remove them. |
| --- |

| ▪  **Tripwire: **Placed across a 10ft gap. The first enemy to cross it must succeed on an Agility Save against Difficulty 5 or be knocked Prone, and you generate 1 Guile. |
| --- |

| ▪  **Smoke Pellet: **Thrown within 20ft as part of deploying this trap. Creates a 10ft radius cloud of smoke until the start of your next turn. All creatures within the cloud are Blinded. |
| --- |

| **Trap Mastery  │  Level 6  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| Your traps become more sophisticated and harder to detect. Enemies cannot identify your traps through casual observation: they require a Difficulty 7 Focus Save to spot them before triggering. Additionally, when an enemy triggers one of your traps, you immediately generate 1 Guile and may make one ranged attack against that enemy as a Free Action. You generate this Guile and make this Free-Action attack at most once per turn, no matter how many of your traps are triggered. |
| --- |

| **Kill Zone  │  Level 9  │  Standard Action  │  Cost: 3 Guile** |
| --- |

| You rapidly deploy all three trap types simultaneously in a 20ft radius area centered on a point within 30ft. All three traps activate at once: the area becomes Difficult Terrain from caltrops, tripwires crisscross it forcing Saves for any creature moving more than 5ft within the zone, and smoke fills the perimeter. Any enemy that enters or starts their turn in the Kill Zone must resolve all applicable trap effects. The deployed traps persist until triggered or until combat ends, as normal for your traps. |
