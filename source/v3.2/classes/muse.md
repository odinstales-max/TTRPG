**THE MUSE**

[Game Name]  │  Class

*"**The Muse does not win battles by hitting hardest. They win by ensuring every ally hits harder, every enemy hesitates, and the emotional fabric of the engagement bends toward the outcome they have already decided on. To fight alongside a Muse is to feel inexplicably more capable. To fight against one is to feel, just at the wrong moment, uncertain.**"*

**CLASS CHASSIS**

| **Health Tier** | Skirmisher: 7 + Max Vigor Die at Level 1. Roll your Vigor Die and add the result to your maximum HP at each level after 1st. |
| --- | --- |
| **Armor** | Light Armor, Arcane Vestments. |
| **Weapons** | Simple Weapons, Finesse Weapons, Arcane Armaments. |
| **Starting Dice** | Focus d6, Agility d6, Resolve d4, Vigor d4. |
| **Daily Reserves** | +3 Focus Points and +1 Agility Point per Level. |
| **Spellcasting Schools** | Resonance, Illusion, Divination. |

**Starting Equipment**

▪  A Rapier (Finesse, Piercing) or a Hand Crossbow and 20 Bolts.

▪  Performer's Leathers (Light Armor: +1 Vigor Defense, +1 Agility Defense) or Arcane Vestments (Focus Defense +1, Resolve Defense +2).

▪  An Instrument or Performance Focus (serves as Arcane Focus for Muse spells).

▪  A Performer's Pack (Costume pieces, fine ink, correspondence paper, rations, bedroll).

If building from scratch: 100 Gold and the proficiencies listed above.

**CLASS FEATURES**

| **The Inspiration Engine  │  Level 1  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| You feed on the energy of success and emotional resonance. You track a pool of creative and psychic energy points called Acclaim. Your maximum Acclaim capacity starts at 2 and scales to 5 at Level 10 (Maestro raises this to 7). You begin every combat with 0 Acclaim. Acclaim fades to 0 when combat ends or if you fall Incapacitated. You also gain Dancer's Step (Quick Action │ Cost: 1 Agility Point): as a Quick Action, spend 1 Agility Point to move up to half your Base Speed without provoking Opportunity Attacks. |
| --- |

| **Generating Acclaim** |
| --- |

| ▪  **Inspired Success: **Whenever an ally hits their target on a roll that was Upgraded by any of your features, you generate 1 Acclaim. |
| --- |

| **Sharp Timing  │  Level 1  │  Reaction  │  Cost: 1 Acclaim** |
| --- |

| ***Trigger: ****An enemy within 60ft makes an Impact Roll.* |
| --- |

| As a Reaction, when an enemy within 60ft that can hear you makes an Impact Roll, spend 1 Acclaim to deliver a precisely timed verbal strike at the worst possible moment. The enemy's Impact Die is Downgraded. |
| --- |

| **Rallying Performance  │  Level 1  │  Quick Action  │  Cost: 1 Focus Point** |
| --- |

| You deliver a burst of emotional inspiration to one ally within 60ft who can hear you. That ally Upgrades their next Impact Die or Save Die: their choice, declared before they roll. This Upgrade applies to one die roll of their choosing made before the start of your next turn. If they hit using the Upgraded die, you generate 1 Acclaim from Inspired Success. |
| --- |

| **Adaptive Response  │  Level 2  │  Quick Action  │  Cost: None** |
| --- |

| Your agility is matched only by your opportunism. You may Dash, Disengage from melee without triggering Opportunity Attacks, or attempt to Hide as a Quick Action on your turn. This does not consume your Standard Action. To Hide successfully, you must move to a position where at least one solid object fully breaks line of sight between you and the observing creature. |
| --- |

| **Psychic Jab  │  Level 2  │  Standard Action  │  Cost: 0 Focus Points** |
| --- |

| You fire a concentrated psychic needle at one target within 60ft. Roll your Focus Die against the target's Resolve Defense. On a hit, deal Psychic damage equal to your Focus Die. At Level 5, Psychic Jab rolls two Focus Dice, and at Level 9, three Focus Dice. Psychic Jab costs no Focus. If Psychic Jab's Impact Die Explodes, the target is also Dazed until the end of their next turn. |
| --- |

| **Ability Die Increase  │  Levels 3, 5, 7, and 9  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| Permanently Upgrade one Attribute Die of your choice by one step on the Step-Die scale. Because your Defense Ratings are derived from your Attribute Dice, improving an Attribute Die simultaneously improves the corresponding Defense Rating baseline. |
| --- |

| **Countercharm  │  Level 4  │  Reaction  │  Cost: 2 Acclaim** |
| --- |

| ***Trigger: ****An ally within 30ft is targeted by a Compulsion or Domination effect, magical or not.* |
| --- |

| As a Reaction, when an ally within 30ft is targeted by any Compulsion or Domination effect, whether magical or not, spend 2 Acclaim to deliver a sharp psychic counter-note. The ally Upgrades their Resolve Die for the Save against that effect. |
| --- |

| **Multiattack  │  Level 5  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| Your combination of blade and psychic precision translates into fluid follow-up attacks. You may attack twice instead of once whenever you take the Attack action on your turn. You may use the Combined Strike rule to pool both attacks into a single Impact Roll against the same target. |
| --- |

| **Blade of the Mind  │  Level 5  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| Your physical and psychic attacks are indistinguishable. Whenever you hit with a melee or ranged weapon attack, you may choose to have the damage dealt as Psychic damage instead of the weapon's normal damage type. This changes only the damage type, for the purposes of resistances and keywords; the attack still targets the weapon's normal Defense, Vigor Defense for a melee weapon or Agility Defense for a ranged weapon. The damage type is always Psychic. |
| --- |

| *Note: Blade of the Mind is what makes the Muse viable as a Skirmisher rather than a pure support caster. Your weapons are psychic delivery systems.* |
| --- |

| **Vitality Surge  │  Levels 5 and 10  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| At Level 5 and again at Level 10, your maximum Hit Points increase by an additional amount equal to the maximum face value of your current Vigor Die, in addition to the standard level-up HP roll. |
| --- |

| **Phantasmal Misdirection  │  Level 7  │  Reaction  │  Cost: 2 Acclaim** |
| --- |

| ***Trigger: ****An enemy targets you with a single-target attack.* |
| --- |

| As a Reaction, when a single-target attack is declared against you, spend 2 Acclaim to project a brief illusory duplicate of yourself stepping in a different direction. The attacker's entire Impact Pool is Downgraded for that attack. If the attack results in a Glancing Blow, they cannot target you again until the start of their next turn: they have committed to the wrong position. |
| --- |

| **Inspiring Surge  │  Level 8  │  Standard Action  │  Cost: 3 Acclaim** |
| --- |

| You deliver a moment of pure, overwhelming inspiration that reshapes the emotional state of every ally within 30ft simultaneously. Each ally within range immediately gains 1 Momentum Token and Upgrades their next Impact Die this round. Additionally, each ally may immediately move up to 10ft as a Free Action as the surge of energy translates into physical urgency. This movement does not trigger Opportunity Attacks. |
| --- |

| **Maestro  │  Level 10  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| You have mastered the art of emotional and tactical orchestration. You gain three permanent benefits. |
| --- |

| First, your Acclaim maximum increases by 2 permanently. |
| --- |

| Second, all your Resonance, Illusion, and Divination spells cost 1 less Focus to a minimum of 0. |
| --- |

| Third, Rallying Performance no longer costs Focus: you may use it freely as a Quick Action with no resource expenditure. Acclaim generated from free uses of Rallying Performance follows normal generation rules. Rallying Performance cannot trigger Encore from itself. |
| --- |

**MUSE****'****S CALLINGS (SUBCLASSES)**

At Level 3, you define the nature of your art and its expression on the battlefield. Your Calling grants features at Levels 3, 6, and 9.

**CALLING OF THE VIRTUOSO**

*The Amplifier: You specialize in making allies extraordinary, turning every Inspiration you deliver into a cascade of compounding advantage.*

| **Resonant Inspiration  │  Level 3  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| Your Rallying Performance echoes. Whenever an ally uses the Upgrade from Rallying Performance and hits their target, they may immediately apply the Upgrade to their next roll of the same type as well: the inspiration persists one extra trigger. This echo can only activate once per Rallying Performance use. |
| --- |

| **Grand Performance  │  Level 6  │  Standard Action  │  Cost: 3 Acclaim** |
| --- |

| You deliver a sweeping performance that elevates every ally simultaneously. All allies within 60ft who can perceive you Upgrade their next Impact Die and their next Save Die. Each Upgrade is independent and can be used on different rolls. |
| --- |

| **Encore  │  Level 9  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| When inspiration lands perfectly, it demands to be repeated. Whenever an ally generates an Explosion on a die that was Upgraded by any of your features, you immediately generate 1 Acclaim and may use Rallying Performance on that same ally again as a Free Action: targeting the same roll or a different one this turn. Encore can trigger at most once per round, and cannot trigger from its own Rallying Performance use. |
| --- |

**CALLING OF THE ILLUSIONIST**

*The Deceiver: You specialize in misdirection, using Illusion magic to make the battlefield look completely different from what it actually is.*

| **Phantom Double  │  Level 3  │  Quick Action  │  Cost: 2 Acclaim** |
| --- |

| You create a convincing illusory duplicate of one ally within 30ft. The duplicate mimics the ally's appearance and movement exactly. Enemies targeting the duplicated ally must first declare which figure they are attacking before rolling. If they choose incorrectly, the duplicate vanishes and they waste the action. To identify the real ally, an enemy must succeed on a Focus Save where the Difficulty equals your Focus Defense. The duplicate lasts until struck, until you create a new one, or for one minute, whichever comes first. Only one Phantom Double may be active at a time. |
| --- |

| **Battlefield Rewrite  │  Level 6  │  Standard Action  │  Cost: 3 Focus Points** |
| --- |

| You overlay the entire visible battlefield with a false version of itself. Walls appear where corridors are, corridors appear where walls stand, allies appear to be in different positions than they occupy. Every enemy must succeed on a Focus Save where the Difficulty equals your Focus Defense or spend their next turn moving in a direction consistent with the false map rather than the real one. Enemies that succeed see through the illusion but cannot automatically warn allies. |
| --- |

| **Perfect Deception  │  Level 9  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| Your illusions achieve startling permanence and depth. The duration of all your Illusion spells is doubled. Enemies that succeed on Saves against your Illusion spells and disbelieve them must still contend with a faint persistent version of the illusion: even knowing it is false, the image is present enough to impose a Downgrade on the first attack they make each round through or into the space the illusion occupies. |
| --- |

**CALLING OF THE SEER**

*The Tactician: You combine Divination mastery with Resonance precision to give your party perfect information and perfect timing.*

| **Combat Intuition  │  Level 3  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| You read the battlefield with preternatural accuracy. You always know the exact Defense Rating values of any creature you have observed for at least one round of combat. This information updates in real time and is automatically shared with all allies within 30ft at the start of each of your turns. |
| --- |

| **Perfect Opening  │  Level 6  │  Reaction  │  Cost: 2 Acclaim** |
| --- |

| ***Trigger: An ally**'**s attack within 60ft results in a Glancing Blow.* |
| --- |

| As a Reaction, when an ally within 60ft makes an attack that results in a Glancing Blow, spend 2 Acclaim to share a flash of precognitive insight. That ally rolls one additional die of the same type as their attack and adds it to the Impact Roll; if the new total exceeds the target's Defense Rating, the attack connects as a full hit. The choice is made after the die is rolled but before damage is applied. If even the new total fails to clear the Defense Rating, the attack remains a Glancing Blow. |
| --- |

| **Foreseen Victory  │  Level 9  │  Standard Action  │  Cost: 4 Acclaim** |
| --- |

| You enter a brief state of perfect tactical precognition for the next round. You observe the most likely actions of every enemy within 60ft and relay this information to all allies simultaneously. Until the start of your next turn, all allies Upgrade their Impact Die on any attack made against an enemy whose action you predicted. All enemies that attack any ally have their Impact Pool Downgraded as your allies anticipate their every move. At the end of this effect you generate 2 Acclaim as the successful prediction resonates with your Inspiration Engine. |
