**THE INVOKER**

[Game Name]  │  Class

*"**The Invoker does not merely believe in a higher power. They are in active dialogue with it. Every ward they raise, every command they speak, every enemy they scour with radiant light is a sentence in an ongoing conversation between themselves and something vast. They are divine authority made tactical.**"*

**CLASS CHASSIS**

| **Health Tier** | Fragile Caster: 5 + Max Vigor Die at Level 1. Roll your Vigor Die and add the result to your maximum HP at each level after 1st. |
| --- | --- |
| **Armor** | Light Armor, Arcane Vestments. |
| **Weapons** | Simple Weapons, Sacred Armaments (Maces, Flails, Divine Focuses). |
| **Starting Dice** | Focus d6, Resolve d6, Vigor d4, Agility d4. |
| **Daily Reserves** | +3 Focus Points and +1 Resolve Point per Level. |
| **Spellcasting Schools** | Abjuration, Resonance, Divination. Radiant damage comes from the Invoker's own class features, not from a school. |

**Starting Equipment**

▪  A Sacred Mace (Bludgeoning) or a Radiant Flail (Bludgeoning, Vicious on Radiant damage).

▪  Light Vestments (+1 Vigor Defense, Focus Defense +1, Resolve Defense +1) or Arcane Vestments (Focus Defense +2, Resolve Defense +1).

▪  A Divine Focus (Holy Symbol, Reliquary, or Sacred Tome).

▪  A Priest's Pack (Bedroll, Rations, Candles, Bandages, Prayer Book).

If building from scratch: 100 Gold and the proficiencies listed above.

**CLASS FEATURES**

| **The Divine Accord  │  Level 1  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| You maintain an active covenant with a divine presence that rewards acts of protection and holy authority. You track a pool of divine energy points called Accord. Your maximum Accord capacity starts at 2 and scales to 5 at Level 10 (Voice of the Divine raises this to 7). You begin every combat with 0 Accord. Accord fades to 0 when combat ends or if you fall Incapacitated. You also gain Steadfast Prayer (Quick Action │ Cost: 1 Resolve Point): as a Quick Action, spend 1 Resolve Point to grant yourself or an ally within 30ft an Upgrade on their next Save. |
| --- |

| **Generating Accord** |
| --- |

| ▪  **Act of Protection: **Whenever you cast an Abjuration or Divination spell that protects an ally or removes a condition, you generate 1 Accord. |
| --- |

| **Sacred Bolt  │  Level 1  │  Standard Action  │  Cost: 0 Focus Points** |
| --- |

| You hurl a bolt of condensed divine authority at one target within 60ft. Roll your Focus Die against the target's Resolve Defense. On a hit, deal Radiant damage equal to your Focus Die. At Level 5, Sacred Bolt rolls two Focus Dice. As a zero-cost attack it is capped at two dice and scales no further: it is a reliable baseline, not a replacement for spending Focus on higher-Tier spells. Sacred Bolt costs no Focus, making it your reliable zero-cost attack. |
| --- |

| **Divine Ward  │  Level 1  │  Reaction  │  Cost: 1 Accord** |
| --- |

| ***Trigger: ****An ally within 30ft is targeted by an attack.* |
| --- |

| As a Reaction, when an ally within 30ft is targeted by an attack, spend 1 Accord to wrap them in a brief divine barrier. The attacker's entire Impact Pool is Downgraded for that single attack. |
| --- |

| **Aura of Clarity  │  Level 2  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| Your divine presence cuts through confusion and sensory interference. All allies within 15ft of you Upgrade their Save Die against any effect that would apply the Blinded or Silenced condition. This aura is always active while you are conscious. At Level 9, the radius extends to 30ft and allies within the aura become immune to the Blinded and Silenced conditions. |
| --- |

| **Healing Word  │  Level 2  │  Quick Action  │  Cost: 1 Focus Point** |
| --- |

| You speak a brief divine word of restoration to one ally within 60ft. They immediately roll their Vigor Die and regain that many Hit Points. Because this is a Quick Action, it can be used in the same turn as a Standard Action spell or attack, making it one of the Invoker's most tactically flexible tools. Casting Healing Word generates 1 Accord from Act of Protection. Healing Word cannot be used to heal yourself. |
| --- |

| **Ability Die Increase  │  Levels 3, 5, 7, and 9  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| Permanently Upgrade one Attribute Die of your choice by one step on the Step-Die scale. Because your Defense Ratings are derived from your Attribute Dice, improving an Attribute Die simultaneously improves the corresponding Defense Rating baseline. |
| --- |

| **Denounce  │  Level 4  │  Standard Action  │  Cost: 2 Accord** |
| --- |

| You level divine judgment at one target within 60ft, marking them as an enemy of your covenant. Roll your Resolve Die against the target's Resolve Defense. On a hit, the target is Denounced for 1 minute. While Denounced: all Radiant damage they take is increased by a roll of your Focus Die (rolled once when Denounce lands and applied as a fixed amount for the duration), and all allies can see a visible divine brand on them. Additionally, the target cannot benefit from healing of any kind until the end of their next turn. On a Glancing Blow, the target cannot benefit from healing until the end of their next turn only. |
| --- |

| **Sacred Overflow  │  Level 5  │  Standard Action  │  Cost: 3 Accord** |
| --- |

| You open yourself fully as a conduit for divine power, releasing it in a wave of purifying energy. Choose one of the following effects. |
| --- |

| **Radiant Wave: **Roll your Focus Die against the Resolve Defense of all enemies within 30ft. On a hit, deal Radiant damage equal to 2 Focus Dice and each target is Dazed until the end of their next turn. |
| --- |

| **Mass Restoration: **All allies within 30ft immediately roll their Vigor Die and regain that many Hit Points. Additionally, remove one condition of your choice from each ally: choose from Bleeding, Blinded, Dazed, Silenced, or Prone. |
| --- |

| **Vitality Surge  │  Levels 5 and 10  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| At Level 5 and again at Level 10, your maximum Hit Points increase by an additional amount equal to the maximum face value of your current Vigor Die, in addition to the standard level-up HP roll. |
| --- |

| **Divine Bulwark  │  Level 7  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| Your covenant extends physical protection to those near you. All allies within 15ft of you gain +1 Vigor Defense and +1 Agility Defense. This passive aura represents the lingering divine authority that surrounds you at all times. Because it is a flat Defense bonus rather than a Downgrade, it stacks normally with Divine Ward: an ally within the aura who is also shielded by Divine Ward benefits from both at once. |
| --- |

| *Note: Divine Bulwark transforms the Invoker into a zone of heightened protection. Standing next to an Invoker should feel materially safer than standing anywhere else on the battlefield.* |
| --- |

| **Word of Recall  │  Level 8  │  Quick Action  │  Cost: 3 Accord** |
| --- |

| You speak a single word of divine recall that instantly moves one ally from anywhere in the combat to an unoccupied space adjacent to you. The ally teleports instantly. This movement does not trigger Opportunity Attacks. Word of Recall can extract an ally from a Grapple, from inside a hazardous zone, or from any position on the battlefield regardless of distance or obstacles. It cannot move an ally across planar boundaries. |
| --- |

| **Voice of the Divine  │  Level 10  │  Passive (Command: Standard Action, Once Per Combat)** |
| --- |

| Your connection to the divine reaches its ultimate expression. You gain three permanent benefits. |
| --- |

| First, your Accord maximum increases by 2 permanently. |
| --- |

| Second, all your Abjuration spells cost 1 less Focus to a minimum of 0. |
| --- |

| Third, once per combat as a Standard Action, you may speak a divine command to one creature within 60ft that automatically succeeds: no roll required. The command must be a single simple physical action achievable in one round such as move, drop a held item, or stand. It cannot force self-harm, spell casting, use of class features, or any action requiring the target's willing participation. The creature executes it on their next turn. |
| --- |

**INVOCATION ORDERS (SUBCLASSES)**

At Level 3, you define your divine specialization. Your Order grants features at Levels 3, 6, and 9.

**ORDER OF THE AEGIS**

*The Shield: You are pure protection. Your covenant is with the defended, not the attacker.*

| **Layered Ward  │  Level 3  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| Your Divine Ward grows more powerful. Whenever you use Divine Ward, the Downgrade applies to the attacker's entire Impact Pool and the benefit persists for that attacker's next attack against the protected ally as well: not just the single triggering attack. The attacker must work twice as hard to breach your protection. |
| --- |

| **Sacred Sanctuary  │  Level 6  │  Standard Action  │  Cost: 2 Accord** |
| --- |

| You designate a 15ft radius area as sanctified ground centered on a point within 30ft. While inside the sanctuary: all allies have all incoming Impact Rolls Downgraded, no ally can be forcibly moved out of the sanctuary against their will, and the Healing Word feature heals an additional amount equal to your Resolve Die within the sanctuary. The sanctuary lasts 1 minute or until you move more than 60ft from its center. Sacred Sanctuary's Downgrade does not stack with Divine Ward against the same attack: apply the greater effect. Divine Bulwark, being a flat Defense bonus, stacks with it normally. |
| --- |

| **Unassailable  │  Level 9  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| Your wards become nearly impossible to overcome. Whenever a protected ally would take damage that reduces them to 0 Hit Points, they instead drop to 1 Hit Point. This triggers automatically once per combat per ally: each ally can only benefit from Unassailable once per combat. |
| --- |

**ORDER OF THE INQUISITION**

*The Purger: You specialize in identifying and destroying supernatural threats, wielding divine authority as a weapon against the unclean.*

| **True Sight  │  Level 3  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| Your divine perception pierces all concealment. You can always see Invisible creatures, see through Illusions as if you had successfully disbelieved them, and immediately know when a creature within 30ft is under the effects of magical mind control or Compulsion. Creatures under Compulsion or Domination are visible to you as having a distinct divine signature even if they appear normal to everyone else. |
| --- |

| **Brand of Censure  │  Level 6  │  Standard Action  │  Cost: 2 Accord** |
| --- |

| You mark one target within 60ft with a divine brand of judgment. While branded: that target cannot use teleportation or planar movement abilities, all Divination and Abjuration spells targeting the brand reveal its exact position to all allies regardless of concealment, and the first time each round that an ally hits the branded target, you generate 1 Accord. The brand persists until the target is reduced to 0 HP. |
| --- |

| **Divine Execution  │  Level 9  │  Standard Action  │  Cost: 4 Accord** |
| --- |

| You channel concentrated divine judgment into a single obliterating strike. This is a Visceral strike. Roll your Focus Die against the target's Resolve Defense to connect; on a hit, deal 3 Focus Dice of Radiant damage as True Damage, and an additional 2 Focus Dice if the target is Denounced. On a Glancing Blow the strike deals no damage, but the target is Dazed until the end of their next turn and you gain 1 Momentum. |
| --- |

**ORDER OF THE PROPHET**

*The Seer: You use divine foresight and Divination mastery to reshape tactical reality before the battle has even fully begun.*

| **Forewarned  │  Level 3  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| You cannot be surprised. At the start of each combat, before Initiative is rolled, you may immediately observe the opening positions of all enemies and share this information freely with allies. This observation does not consume any actions and does not negate enemy ambush positioning: it only reveals where enemies are standing at the start of the encounter. |
| --- |

| **Prophetic Strike  │  Level 6  │  Reaction  │  Cost: 2 Accord** |
| --- |

| ***Trigger: When an ally**'**s attack results in a Glancing Blow.* |
| --- |

| As a Reaction, when an ally within 60ft makes an attack that results in a Glancing Blow, spend 2 Accord to share a flash of prophetic vision with them. That ally rolls one additional die of the same type as their attack and adds it to the Impact Roll; if the new total exceeds the target's Defense Rating, the attack connects as a full hit. The choice is made after the die is rolled but before damage is calculated. If even the new total fails to clear the Defense Rating, the attack remains a Glancing Blow. |
| --- |

| **Temporal Decree  │  Level 9  │  Standard Action  │  Cost: 4 Accord** |
| --- |

| You invoke a divine decree that reshapes the immediate future for one target within 60ft. Roll your Resolve Die against the target's Resolve Defense. On a hit, you declare one of the following decrees to take effect on the target's next turn. |
| --- |

| **Decree of Stillness: **On the target's next turn it is Dazed (it loses its Reaction and the Impact Die for its next attack is Downgraded) and it cannot take Quick Actions. It retains its Standard Action and full movement. |
| --- |

| **Decree of Confusion: **The target must attack the nearest creature to them regardless of allegiance. |
| --- |

| **Decree of Revelation: **The target becomes Visible, loses all defensive bonuses from Cover and concealment, and all attacks against them are Upgraded until the start of their following turn. |
| --- |

| On a Glancing Blow, the target is Dazed until the end of their next turn. |
