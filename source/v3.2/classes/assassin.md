**THE ASSASSIN**

Era of Silence  │  Class

*"**The target never sees the Assassin coming. That is not luck. That is architecture. Every position chosen, every condition applied, every breath timed to the gap between heartbeats: all of it is construction. The kill itself is just the last brick.**"*

**CLASS FEATURES**

| **The Predator's Edge  │  Level 1  │  Passive** |

| --- |

| Assassination is not rage. It is patience made violent. You track a pool of precision energy called Lethality. Your maximum Lethality capacity starts at 2 and scales to 5 at Level 10. You begin every combat with 0 Lethality. Lethality fades to 0 when combat ends or if you fall Incapacitated. Lethality is generated through positioning and exploitation, not attrition. Two further options sharpen the kill. Slip (Quick Action │ Cost: 1 Agility Point): as a Quick Action, spend 1 Agility Point to move up to 10ft without provoking Opportunity Attacks; if you end this movement adjacent to an enemy, your next Impact Die against it this turn is Upgraded. Pinpoint (Free Action │ Cost: 1 Focus Point): as a Free Action on your turn, spend 1 Focus Point to Upgrade your next Impact Die this turn as you read the target's anatomy. |

| --- |

| **Generating Lethality** |

| --- |

| ▪  **From the Dark: **Whenever you hit an enemy while you have Cover, are Hidden, or the enemy has not yet acted this round, you generate 1 Lethality. |

| --- |

| --- |

| --- |

| **The First Strike  │  Level 1  │  Passive** |

| --- |

| The Assassin's opening blow is not a gamble. The position was chosen, the moment was selected, the target was compromised before they knew anyone was there. The preparation is the guarantee. |

| --- |

| Whenever you make your first attack of combat from a Hidden position or against a target that has not yet acted this combat, that attack automatically triggers an Explosion. The initial maximum is treated as given: roll the Impact Die one time and add the result to your damage total. This bonus die does not explode again even if it rolls its own maximum. The Explosion occurs without spending Lethality. If your weapon has the Vicious property, this single bonus die is still Upgraded by one step before it is rolled. |

| --- |

| The First Strike also immediately triggers Arterial Precision's Bleeding application if it has been unlocked, and fires any toxin Explosion triggers through Toxic Rupture if applicable. |

| --- |

| *Note: The First Strike fires once per combat. It applies on the first qualifying attack regardless of round number: if you spend the opening round repositioning and strike on round two from Hidden, The First Strike still applies.* |

| --- |

| **Executioner's Strike  │  Level 1  │  Free Action  │  Cost: 1 Lethality** |

| --- |

| ***Trigger: ****When you hit an enemy with an Agility-based attack.* |

| --- |

| You have identified the precise fault line in your target's defenses and driven your strike directly through it. Spend 1 Lethality to forgo rolling your Impact Die entirely. Instead, treat the die as though it rolled its maximum face value. This maximum result triggers a single Explosion: roll the Impact Die one time and add the result to the maximum. This bonus die does not explode again even if it rolls its own maximum. If your weapon has the Vicious property, this single bonus die is still Upgraded by one step before it is rolled. |

| --- |

| A d8 becomes 8 + (roll d8 once). A d10 becomes 10 + (roll d10 once). The ceiling is fixed and the bonus is a single die. At Levels 1 through 4, Executioner's Strike may be used once per combat. From Level 5 onward, it may be used once per turn regardless of Multiattack. Also from Level 5 onward, you may spend 1 additional Lethality (2 total) to make the strike Visceral instead. Guaranteed maximum and Visceral are mutually exclusive: when you make the strike Visceral you do not take the maximum face value but instead roll your Impact Die normally (it may Explode on its own), and on a hit the full rolled damage is dealt as True Damage, bypassing the Defense Rating entirely. You choose the guaranteed maximum or the Visceral bypass, never both. |

| --- |

| **Slip Away  │  Level 1  │  Quick Action  │  Cost: None** |

| --- |

| You are trained to not be where the enemy swings. As a Quick Action on your turn, choose one of the following. |

| --- |

| ▪  **Disengage: **Move up to 15ft without triggering Opportunity Attacks. This movement generates 1 Lethality if you leave melee range of at least one enemy. |

| --- |

| ▪  **Vanish: **If you are adjacent to a wall, pillar, heavy cover, or any terrain feature that could plausibly conceal you, you become Hidden. You remain Hidden until you attack, cast a spell, or move into an open area with no concealment. |

| --- |

| ▪  **Redirect: **Force one enemy within 5ft that is currently making an Opportunity Attack against an ally to redirect that attack to you instead. If the attack hits you, you generate 1 Lethality from the contact. |

| --- |

| *Note: Slip Away costs no Reserves and does not consume your Standard Action. It is available every turn at no cost.* |

| --- |

| **Read the Wound  │  Level 2  │  Passive** |

| --- |

| You have studied anatomy the way a smith studies metal: not to appreciate it, but to know exactly where it fails. Whenever you deal damage to an enemy with an Agility-based attack, you immediately know that enemy's current HP state: full, wounded (below half HP), or critical (below one-quarter HP). Additionally, you always know which Defense Rating of the target is currently lowest. Read the Wound does not require an action or a roll. The information is automatic on any hit. |

| --- |

| **Arterial Precision  │  Level 2  │  Passive** |

| --- |

| Your strikes are anatomically deliberate. Whenever your Impact Die triggers an Explosion (whether naturally or through Executioner's Strike) the target begins Bleeding. A Bleeding target takes 2 True Damage at the start of each of their turns. Bleeding persists until the target or an ally spends a Standard Action to clear it. |

| --- |

| A target already Bleeding when you trigger another Explosion does not stack a second Bleed: instead, the existing Bleed deals 2 additional True Damage per turn, to the standard maximum of 6, for the remainder of combat. |

| --- |

| Additionally, the Explosion that triggered this feature Sunders the target's Vigor Defense by 1 until the end of your next turn. Vigor Defense cannot be reduced below 0. |

| --- |

| **Ability Die Increase  │  Levels 3, 5, 7, and 9  │  Passive** |

| --- |

| Permanently Upgrade one Attribute Die of your choice by one step on the Step-Die scale. Because your Defense Ratings are derived from your Attribute Dice, improving an Attribute Die simultaneously improves the corresponding Defense Rating baseline. |

| --- |

| **Toxicologist  │  Level 4  │  Passive  │  Crafting** |

| --- |

| You have learned to synthesize industrial-grade toxins from materials scavenged in the ruins. When you complete a Long Rest, you may craft a number of toxin vials equal to the maximum face value of your current Focus Die. Applying a toxin to your weapon or a piece of ammunition is a Free Action. When you hit a target with a poisoned weapon, the toxin takes effect. It persists until the target spends a Standard Action to clear it from their system. |

| --- |

| ▪  **Crippling Oil: **The target's Speed is halved. |

| --- |

| ▪  **Wither-Blood: **The target takes 3 True Damage at the start of each of their turns. This effect stacks up to two times if reapplied. |

| --- |

| ▪  **Mind-Fog: **The target is Dazed: the Impact Die for their next attack is Downgraded and they cannot take Reactions until the start of their next turn. |

| --- |

| ▪  **Flash-Powder (Thrown, 30ft): **Instead of coating a blade, this is thrown. Roll your Agility Die against the target's Agility Defense. On a hit, the target is Blinded until the end of their next turn. On a Glancing Blow, the target's next Impact Die is Downgraded. |

| --- |

| *Note: Toxins are not spells and do not cost Focus. They are physical compounds applied to physical weapons. Any class feature that specifies attack or hit triggers toxin delivery.* |

| --- |

| **Multiattack  │  Level 5  │  Passive** |

| --- |

| Your precision extends to rapid follow-through. You may attack twice instead of once whenever you take the Attack action on your turn. Executioner's Strike may only be used once per turn regardless of Multiattack. |

| --- |

| **Thrill of the Kill  │  Level 5  │  Passive** |

| --- |

| Precision under pressure is its own fuel. Whenever your Impact Die triggers a natural Explosion you immediately generate 1 Lethality. Explosions produced by Executioner's Strike or The First Strike are not natural and do not generate Lethality. Additionally, whenever you reduce an enemy to 0 Hit Points, you generate 1 Lethality. |

| --- |

| **Vitality Surge  │  Levels 5 and 10  │  Passive** |

| --- |

| At Level 5 and again at Level 10, your maximum Hit Points increase by an additional amount equal to the maximum face value of your current Vigor Die, in addition to the standard level-up HP roll. |

| --- |

| **Ghost Step  │  Level 7  │  Reaction  │  Cost: 3 Agility Points** |

| --- |

| ***Trigger: ****You are hit by any attack that targets your Agility Defense or that originates from an area-of-effect.* |

| --- |

| As a Reaction, spend 3 Agility Points to negate all damage from the triggering attack and immediately move up to 10ft in any direction. This movement does not trigger Opportunity Attacks. If this movement takes you into Cover or a concealed position, you immediately become Hidden. Ghost Step only triggers on Agility Defense attacks and area effects: the kinds of damage that punish being in the wrong position. |

| --- |

| **Perfect Form  │  Level 8  │  Passive** |

| --- |

| Your strikes have ceased to be improvised and become architectural. Whenever your Impact Die triggers an Explosion, roll the Explosion die with your Impact Die Upgraded by one step. If that Upgraded die also rolls its maximum, the next die in the chain is Upgraded again. Each die in a single Explosion chain may be Upgraded once this way: the Upgrade does not compound beyond the chain. |

| --- |

| Additionally, when Perfect Form's Upgraded Explosion die also Explodes, the target is knocked Prone by the force of the hit in addition to taking damage. |

| --- |

| *Note: A d8 Impact Die now Explodes into a d10 bonus roll. A d10 Explodes into a d12. A d12 Explodes into a d12 again: it is already at the ceiling but can still continue to chain.* |

| --- |

| **The Final Cut  │  Level 10  │  Passive  │  Once Per Combat** |

| --- |

| You have reached the end of the process. Every element of the Assassin's craft operates in concert. |

| --- |

| Once per combat when you trigger Executioner's Strike, you may declare The Final Cut. Every condition currently applied to the target by you resolves instantly and simultaneously at the moment of the strike, before the strike's damage is calculated. Each condition fires its per-turn effect three times at once: Bleeding deals its True Damage three times, each damaging toxin fires its effect three times, and so on for any other recurring condition you have applied. All Sunders you have applied to the target become permanent for the remainder of combat. After resolution, all conditions remain active and continue their normal timing from this point forward. (Multiplier tunable.) |

| --- |

| The Final Cut accelerates conditions. It does not consume them. |

| --- |

| Additionally, at Level 10 the first Executioner's Strike you use each combat costs 0 Lethality. |

**ASSASSIN****'****S VEILS (SUBCLASSES)**

At Level 3, you define the method through which your precision manifests. Your Veil grants features at Levels 3, 6, and 9.

**VEIL OF THE NIGHTSHADE**

*The Poisoner: You do not end fights with a single blow. You end them before they start, with compounds that dissolve the body**'**s ability to resist.*

| **Compound Formula  │  Level 3  │  Passive** |

| --- |

| Your knowledge of toxin chemistry deepens. When you craft toxin vials during a Long Rest, you may permanently combine any two toxin effects into a single vial. A compound vial delivers both effects on a single hit. The two effects resolve independently and stack normally. Additionally, your crafting output increases: you now craft toxin vials equal to twice the maximum face value of your Focus Die rather than once. |

| --- |

| **Toxic Rupture  │  Level 3  │  Passive** |

| --- |

| Your toxins are volatile enough that the physical trauma of an Explosion forces them through the bloodstream at catastrophic speed. Whenever your Impact Die triggers an Explosion against a target currently suffering from any of your toxins, the toxin's effect immediately triggers a second time: resolving its full damage or condition instantly as though applied again at this moment. |

| --- |

| If the toxin is Wither-Blood, the target takes the True Damage instantly rather than waiting for their next turn. If it is Crippling Oil, their Speed drops to 0 for this turn. If it is Mind-Fog, they immediately lose their Reaction for the rest of this round. Toxic Rupture's second trigger also advances the Wither-Blood stack by 1 if already active, up to the maximum of 2. |

| --- |

| **Noxious Cloud  │  Level 6  │  Standard Action  │  Cost: 2 Lethality** |

| --- |

| You throw a volatile chemical charge to a point within 30ft. A 20ft radius cloud of aerosolized toxin erupts and persists until the start of your next turn. Roll your Focus Die against the Agility Defense of all enemies inside the cloud at the time of detonation. On a hit, each affected target takes Poison damage equal to your Focus Die and immediately suffers the Wither-Blood toxin effect. On a Glancing Blow, the target takes Poison damage equal to your Focus Die only. Allies inside the cloud at detonation are unaffected. The cloud does not block line of sight. |

| --- |

| *Note: If any die rolled for the detonation Explodes, the cloud lingers until the end of your next turn instead of the start, and any enemy that enters the cloud area for the first time on their turn immediately suffers a Wither-Blood application with no roll required.* |

| --- |

| **Master Poisoner  │  Level 9  │  Passive** |

| --- |

| Your toxins have been refined to attack the nervous system directly. Any enemy currently suffering from at least one of your toxins has their Agility Defense and Focus Defense each permanently Sundered by 1 for the remainder of combat. Neither can be reduced below 0. This Sunder is reapplied each time a new toxin is applied: if the enemy clears their toxins and you reapply one, the Sunder reactivates. Additionally, the Standard Action required to clear your toxins now requires a successful Focus Save against Difficulty 5 first. On a failure, the action is spent and the toxin remains. |

| --- |

**VEIL OF THE RAZORWIRE**

*The Bladeworker: You do not hide between strikes. You are never far enough away to be safe, and neither is the enemy.*

| **Pressure Point  │  Level 3  │  Free Action  │  Cost: 1 Lethality** |

| --- |

| ***Trigger: ****When you hit an enemy with a Finesse weapon.* |

| --- |

| You drive the strike into a precise anatomical location. Spend 1 Lethality when you hit with a Finesse weapon to apply one of the following effects. Pressure Point does not replace your damage roll: it applies in addition to the hit's full damage. |

| --- |

| ▪  **Joint Strike: **The target's next Impact Die is Downgraded as the limb bearing their weapon or dominant arm is compromised. |

| --- |

| ▪  **Balance Break: **The target is knocked Prone. |

| --- |

| ▪  **Throat Cut: **The target cannot take Reactions until the start of their next turn. |

| --- |

| **Thread the Needle  │  Level 3  │  Passive** |

| --- |

| Your movement through contested space is unnervingly precise. Whenever you move through an enemy's melee reach without triggering an Opportunity Attack (through Slip Away or any other means) your next Impact Die before the end of your turn is Upgraded. Additionally, whenever you make a Finesse attack against an enemy that is adjacent to at least one of your allies, your Impact Die is Upgraded. Both Upgrades stack if both conditions are met. |

| --- |

| **Blade Cage  │  Level 6  │  Reaction  │  Cost: 2 Lethality** |

| --- |

| ***Trigger: ****An enemy within your melee reach attempts to move away from you.* |

| --- |

| As a Reaction, when an adjacent enemy attempts to leave your melee reach by any means (movement, teleportation, or forced displacement) spend 2 Lethality to make one immediate Finesse attack against them. This attack has its Impact Die Upgraded. If the attack hits, the target's movement is halted: they do not complete the move. On a Glancing Blow, the target takes damage but completes half their intended movement. |

| --- |

| **Anatomical Mastery  │  Level 9  │  Passive** |

| --- |

| Every enemy you fight has a blueprint you have now memorized. Whenever you use Pressure Point, you may apply two effects instead of one for the cost of 1 Lethality. The two effects are chosen simultaneously and resolve at the same moment. |

| --- |

| Additionally, whenever you hit with a Finesse attack, the target's Vigor Defense is Sundered by 2 for that single attack's damage calculation. This Sunder applies before the Defense Rating comparison that determines hit or Glancing Blow. Vigor Defense cannot be reduced below 0 by this effect. (Magnitude tunable.) |

| --- |

**VEIL OF THE DEADEYE**

*The Sniper: You are not in the fight. You are watching the fight from a position the fight cannot reach.*

| **First Round Priority  │  Level 3  │  Passive** |

| --- |

| You have already chosen your position before combat begins. Whenever you roll Initiative, Upgrade your Agility Die. On your first turn of each combat, any enemy that has not yet acted this round counts as unaware of your presence for the purpose of generating Lethality, even if they are actively watching for threats. |

| --- |

| *Note: Combined with The First Strike, the opening shot on the first round is a guaranteed Explosion followed by an automatic Lethality generation from From the Dark. The Deadeye enters the fight fully primed.* |

| --- |

| **Overpenetration  │  Level 3  │  Passive** |

| --- |

| Your ranged Impact Die is weighted and propelled with enough force to punch through material resistance. Whenever your Impact Die triggers an Explosion on a ranged attack, the projectile passes through the primary target and strikes the enemy directly behind them in the line of fire. That secondary target takes Piercing damage equal to the result of the Explosion die with no roll required. If there is no enemy in the direct line behind the primary target, the Explosion die damage is simply lost. |

| --- |

| *Note: If the secondary target is also a valid enemy and the Explosion die rolled its maximum, the penetration continues to a third target in the same line. The third target takes Piercing damage equal to your Agility Die.* |

| --- |

| **Suppressing Volley  │  Level 6  │  Standard Action  │  Cost: 2 Lethality** |

| --- |

| You fire a high-velocity spread of bolts or arrows in a 40ft line originating from your position. Roll your Agility Die against the Vigor Defense of all enemies in the line. On a hit, each target takes Piercing damage equal to your Agility Die. On a Glancing Blow, each target's Speed is reduced to 0 until the end of their next turn. Suppressing Volley counts as a single attack for the purpose of Multiattack. |

| --- |

| *Note: If any die rolled for this volley Explodes, one target of your choice in the line is also knocked Prone.* |

| --- |

| **Trick Shot  │  Level 9  │  Free Action  │  Cost: 1 Lethality** |

| --- |

| ***Trigger: ****When you hit a target with a ranged Agility-based attack.* |

| --- |

| You have placed the shot with enough precision to dictate what the target does next. Spend 1 Lethality when you hit a target with a ranged attack to apply one of the following effects. |

| --- |

| ▪  **Disarm: **The target immediately drops one held item of your choice. The item lands in their space. |

| --- |

| ▪  **Pin: **The target's Speed is reduced to 0 until the end of their next turn. |

| --- |

| ▪  **Targeting Shot: **Until the end of your next turn, the first ally to attack the target has their Impact Die Upgraded, as your shot has marked the precise vulnerability you are leaving for them. |
