**THE CHARGER**

[Game Name] │ Class

*“****A blur across the battlefield, here and gone before the eye can settle. The Charger does not trade blows. They strike from a place no one expected, leave their mark, and are already somewhere else. Speed is their weapon, their armor, and their answer to every problem on the field.****”*

**CLASS CHASSIS**

| **Health Tier** | Skirmisher: 7 + Max Vigor Die at Level 1. Roll your Vigor Die and add the result to your maximum HP at each level after 1st. |
| --- | --- |
| **Armor** | Light Armor, Medium Armor. |
| **Weapons** | Simple Weapons, Martial Weapons. |
| **Starting Dice** | Agility d6, Vigor d6, Resolve d4, Focus d4. |
| **Daily Reserves** | +3 Agility Points and +1 Vigor Point per Level. |

**Starting Equipment**

▪ A War Spear (Piercing, Reach) or a pair of Short Blades (Slashing, Light).

▪ Reinforced Brigandine (Medium Armor, +2 Vigor Defense, +2 Agility Defense, no Speed penalty).

▪ Four Throwing Knives (Slashing, Thrown, 20ft range).

▪ A Field Pack (Bedroll, Torches, Rations, Bandages, Climbing Rope).

If building from scratch: 100 Gold and the proficiencies listed above.

**CLASS FEATURES**

| **Unstoppable Engine │ Level 1 │ Passive** |
| --- |

| You convert motion into power. You track a pool of combat points called Velocity. Your maximum Velocity begins at 2 and rises to 3 at Level 4, 4 at Level 7, and 5 at Level 9. You begin every combat with 0 Velocity, and it fades to 0 when combat ends or if you fall Incapacitated. From your first level you can convert that speed into impact. Kinetic Strike (Free Action │ Cost: 1 Velocity): as a Free Action when you hit a target with a melee attack after moving at least 10ft this turn, spend 1 Velocity to add one Impact Die to that attack, following the Bonus Impact Dice rule. |
| --- |

| **Generating Velocity** |
| --- |

| ▪ **Momentum Gained:** Whenever you move at least 20ft on your turn, you generate 1 Velocity at the end of that movement. |
| --- |

| **Dash │ Level 1 │ Quick Action │ Cost: 1 Agility Point** |
| --- |

| You blur into a second burst of speed. Spend 1 Agility to take your movement action again on your turn. |
| --- |

| **Burst │ Level 1 │ Standard Action │ Cost: 1 Agility Point** |
| --- |

| You attack in a sequence too fast to follow. Spend 1 Agility to make two attacks instead of one. Burst may be used once per combat. At Level 5, Burst makes three attacks instead of two and may be used twice per combat. Burst is the Charger’s opener, not a replacement for the Multiattack gained at Level 5. |
| --- |

| **Quick Charge │ Level 1 │ Quick Action │ Cost: 1 Agility Point** |
| --- |

| You close the gap in an instant. Spend 1 Agility to move up to your Base Speed in a straight line toward an enemy. If you end this movement adjacent to that enemy, your next attack against them this turn adds one Impact Die to its damage. |
| --- |

| **Rapid Recovery │ Level 1 │ Quick Action │ Cost: 1 Vigor Point** |
| --- |

| Your body mends as fast as it moves. Spend 1 Vigor to roll your Vigor Die and regain that many Hit Points. |
| --- |

| **Cutting Slide │ Level 2 │ Standard Action │ Cost: 1 Agility Point** |
| --- |

| You drop into a low slide and cut as you pass. Spend 1 Agility to slide up to 15ft without triggering Opportunity Attacks and strike one enemy you reach. The target takes 1 Impact Die of damage, and you choose one: the target is knocked Prone, or the target is Hamstrung (its Speed is halved and it cannot take Reactions until the end of its next turn). |
| --- |

| **Afterimage │ Level 2 │ Quick Action │ Cost: 2 Velocity** |
| --- |

| You move so quickly you leave a ghost of yourself behind. Spend 2 Velocity. Until the start of your next turn, the first attack made against you is treated as a Glancing Blow: it deals no damage, and any stated Glancing Blow effects apply normally. |
| --- |

| **Bait │ Level 3 │ Quick Action │ Cost: 1 Agility Point** |
| --- |

| You flicker a strike that never lands, and your target commits to a defense against nothing. Spend 1 Agility to target one enemy within reach. That enemy becomes Off-Balance: it acts immediately after its next ally in the turn order rather than at its normal place. If the target has no allies in the combat, it instead acts last in the round. The displacement lasts for one round, after which the target returns to its normal place in the order. |
| --- |

| **Wall Run │ Level 3 │ Movement │ Cost: 1 Agility Point** |
| --- |

| Gravity is a suggestion you outrun. Spend 1 Agility. Until the end of your turn, you may move freely across walls, ceilings, and the surface of water, and you do not trigger Opportunity Attacks from any space you cross during this movement. If you end your turn on a surface that cannot support you, you fall. |
| --- |

| **Phase Step │ Level 4 │ Quick Action │ Cost: 1 Velocity** |
| --- |

| You step through the space between moments. Spend 1 Velocity to instantly move up to 20ft to a space you can see, passing through creatures and solid objects along the way. This movement does not trigger Opportunity Attacks. |
| --- |

| **Cyclone Charge │ Level 5 │ Standard Action │ Cost: 2 Velocity** |
| --- |

| You tear through a line of foes in a single pass. Spend 2 Velocity and move up to your Base Speed in a straight line. Choose up to three enemies whose space you move through or pass adjacent to, and make one attack against each, dealing 2 Impact Dice on a hit. You do not trigger Opportunity Attacks during this movement. |
| --- |

| **Cripple │ Level 5 │ Standard Action │ Cost: 2 Velocity** |
| --- |

| You blow past a target’s guard and leave them open. Spend 2 Velocity and make a melee attack. The target takes 2 Impact Dice of damage, and the next attack made against it before the start of your next turn is Visceral. |
| --- |

| **Multiattack │ Level 5 │ Passive** |
| --- |

| Your speed lets you press an opening twice. You may attack twice instead of once whenever you take the Attack action on your turn. |
| --- |

| **Vitality Surge │ Levels 5 and 10 │ Passive** |
| --- |

| Your body hardens against the punishment of constant motion. At Levels 5 and 10, your maximum Hit Points increase by the maximum face value of your current Vigor Die. |
| --- |

| **Speed Bubble │ Level 6 │ Standard Action │ Cost: 2 Vigor Points** |
| --- |

| You spin fast enough to bend the pace of time in a small space around you. Spend 2 Vigor to create a 10ft radius sphere centered on yourself that lasts until the start of your next turn. Allies who begin their turn inside the sphere double their Speed for that turn. Any enemy that enters the sphere or begins its turn inside is Hamstrung. The sphere moves with you. |
| --- |

| **Trip Line │ Level 6 │ Standard Action │ Cost: 1 Velocity** |
| --- |

| You weave through a crowd, sweeping legs as you pass. Spend 1 Velocity and move up to your Base Speed without triggering Opportunity Attacks. Up to three enemies whose space you move adjacent to during this movement are knocked Prone. |
| --- |

| **Quicksilver Rush │ Level 7 │ Standard Action │ Cost: Velocity** |
| --- |

| You become a streak of motion, scattering everything in your path. Spend Velocity up to your maximum and move up to your Base Speed in a straight line, passing through enemy spaces. Each enemy in your path takes Impact Dice equal to 1 plus the Velocity spent, to a maximum of 4 Impact Dice. You do not trigger Opportunity Attacks during this movement. |
| --- |

| **Unhindered │ Level 7 │ Passive** |
| --- |

| Nothing can pin you down. You are never a valid target for Opportunity Attacks, and you cannot be flanked. |
| --- |

| **Thunderclap Step │ Level 8 │ Standard Action │ Cost: 3 Velocity** |
| --- |

| You land from speed with force enough to crack the ground. Spend 3 Velocity. Every enemy within 10ft of you takes 3 Impact Dice of damage and is knocked Prone. |
| --- |

| **Blur of Motion │ Level 8 │ Passive** |
| --- |

| You live at a tempo others cannot match. You gain a second Quick Action on each of your turns. |
| --- |

| **Ghoststrike │ Level 9 │ Standard Action │ Cost: 2 Vigor Points** |
| --- |

| You strike a single target from every angle at once, a dozen blows landing as one. Spend 2 Vigor and make a melee attack against one target, dealing 4 Impact Dice of damage on a hit. The target loses its next Reaction. |
| --- |

| **Mercurial Healing │ Level 9 │ Passive** |
| --- |

| Your metabolism runs as fast as your feet. Whenever you move at least 20ft on your turn, you regain Hit Points equal to your Vigor Die. Mercurial Healing can trigger no more than three times per combat. |
| --- |

| **The Juggernaut │ Level 10 │ Passive** |
| --- |

| You have outrun the limits that bind everyone else. You cannot be slowed, immobilized, knocked Prone, or stopped by difficult terrain. Additionally, whenever you move 40ft or more on your turn, you may make one additional attack as part of your Attack action that turn, striking a third time on the momentum of your charge. This is the Juggernaut's capstone: repeated impact from impossible speed, a distinct effect rather than more dice piled onto a single blow. (Magnitude tunable.) |
| --- |

**CHARGER WAYS (SUBCLASSES)**

At Level 3, you define your method of speed. Your Way grants features at Levels 3, 6, and 9.

**WAY OF THE DEMOLISHER**

| **Built Momentum │ Level 3 │ Passive** |
| --- |

| Your strikes carry the full weight of your motion. Whenever you have moved at least 20ft on your turn before making an attack, that attack's Impact Roll is Upgraded. Because this is an Upgrade rather than an added die, it stacks with the single bonus die from features like Kinetic Strike instead of competing with it under the one-bonus-die rule. |
| --- |

| **Shockwave │ Level 6 │ Standard Action │ Cost: 2 Velocity** |
| --- |

| You strike the ground with the force of your stored speed, sending a shock through the terrain. Every enemy within 20ft standing on the same surface as you takes 2 Impact Dice of damage. Against any enemy that is also Hamstrung, Pinned, or Prone, this damage increases to 3 Impact Dice. |
| --- |

| **Overrun │ Level 9 │ Passive** |
| --- |

| Your momentum cannot be turned aside. Once per turn, when an attack you make would be reduced to a Glancing Blow, you may spend 1 Velocity to roll one additional die of the same type as the attack and add it to the Impact Roll; if the new total clears the target's Defense Rating, the attack connects as a full hit. If even the new total fails to clear the Defense Rating, the attack remains a Glancing Blow. |
| --- |

**WAY OF THE LINE BREAKER**

| **Opening │ Level 3 │ Passive** |
| --- |

| Your speed tears holes in the enemy line for your allies to pour through. Whenever you knock an enemy Prone using Quicksilver Rush, Trip Line, or Thunderclap Step, each ally that can see the fallen enemy may immediately move up to 10ft toward that enemy as a Reaction. This movement does not trigger Opportunity Attacks. |
| --- |

| **Open the Lane │ Level 6 │ Standard Action │ Cost: 1 Velocity** |
| --- |

| You crash through a cluster of enemies to break their formation apart. Spend 1 Velocity and move up to your Base Speed in a straight line. When you move through or adjacent to a group of three or more enemies, you may push each of them 10ft in a direction of your choice. Enemies pushed into one another take 1 Impact Die of damage and are knocked Prone. You gain 1 Momentum Token for each enemy knocked Prone this way. |
| --- |

| **Slipstream │ Level 9 │ Passive** |
| --- |

| Allies who follow your lead move as fast as you do. Whenever you use Dash, Quick Charge, or Phase Step, one ally you can see may immediately move up to 10ft as a Reaction without triggering Opportunity Attacks. |
| --- |

**WAY OF THE IRONCLAD RUNNER**

| **Second Wind │ Level 3 │ Passive** |
| --- |

| You can keep running long after others would fall. Whenever you would be reduced to 0 Hit Points while you have at least 1 Velocity, you may instead drop to 1 Hit Point and lose all Velocity. This can occur once per combat. |
| --- |

| **Gathering Speed │ Level 6 │ Passive** |
| --- |

| The longer the chase, the harder you hit. For every full 20ft you move on your turn, your next attack that turn has its Impact Roll Upgraded once, to a maximum of two Upgrades. These are Upgrades, not added dice, so they stack with your single bonus die rather than competing with it under the one-bonus-die rule. (Magnitude tunable.) |
| --- |

| **Full Throttle │ Level 9 │ Standard Action │ Cost: Velocity** |
| --- |

| You release all your speed in one catastrophic burst. Once per combat as part of an attack, spend up to 4 Velocity. For each point spent, add 1 Impact Die to that attack’s damage. The target is flung 20ft away and knocked Prone. Full Throttle is the Charger’s one sanctioned exception to the Bonus Impact Dice rule: these are added dice (maximum 4), a once-per-combat Velocity dump on the Furious Strike model, not Upgrade-stacking. |
| --- |
