**THE ZEALOT**

[Game Name]  │  Class

*"**Faith is not a shield. It is a weapon. The Zealot does not pray for deliverance: they deliver it. Armored in conviction and heavy plate, fueled by a spiritual resource that grows with every act of devotion and sacrifice, the Zealot is the most durable and divinely terrifying presence on any battlefield.**"*

**CLASS CHASSIS**

| **Health Tier** | Tank: 11 + Max Vigor Die at Level 1. Roll your Vigor Die and add the result to your maximum HP at each level after 1st. |
| --- | --- |
| **Armor** | Light Armor, Medium Armor, Heavy Armor, Shields. Arcane Vestments available to the Covenant of the Shepherd. |
| **Weapons** | Simple Weapons, Martial Weapons, Sacred Armaments. |
| **Starting Dice** | Vigor d6, Resolve d6, Agility d4, Focus d4. |
| **Daily Reserves** | +3 Vigor Points and +1 Resolve Point per Level. |
| **Spellcasting Schools** | Resonance, Abjuration. Available only to the Doctrine of the Shepherd, which grants spellcasting access at Level 3. Radiant damage comes from the Zealot's own class features, not from a school. |

**Starting Equipment**

▪  A War-Mace (Bludgeoning, Light) and a Kite Shield (+2 Vigor Defense) or a Greatsword (Slashing, Heavy).

▪  Full Plate Armor (+4 Vigor Defense, +4 Agility Defense, Speed -5ft).

▪  A Holy Symbol (Arcane Focus for Zealot spells).

▪  A Crusader's Pack (Bedroll, Rations, Bandages, Torches, Prayer Scroll).

If building from scratch: 120 Gold, and the proficiencies listed above.

**CLASS FEATURES**

| **The Zealous Conviction  │  Level 1  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| Your faith is a physical force that bends the battlefield to your will. You track a pool of divine energy points called Conviction. Your maximum Conviction capacity starts at 2 and scales to 5 at Level 10. You begin every combat with 0 Conviction. Conviction fades to 0 when combat ends or if you fall Incapacitated. You also gain Unbowed (Reaction │ Cost: 1 Vigor Point): as a Reaction when you take damage, spend 1 Vigor Point to roll your Vigor Die and reduce that damage by the result; if this reduces the damage to 0, generate 1 Conviction. You also gain Prayer of Resolve (Quick Action │ Cost: 1 Resolve Point): as a Quick Action, spend 1 Resolve Point to Upgrade your next Save before the start of your next turn, and you are immune to the Frightened condition until then. |
| --- |

| **Generating Conviction** |
| --- |

| ▪  **Righteous Strike: **The first time each turn you deal damage with a melee attack to an enemy, you generate 1 Conviction as your faith surges with each righteous blow. |
| --- |

| **Judgment  │  Level 1  │  Free Action  │  Cost: 1 to 3 Conviction** |
| --- |

| ***Trigger: ****When you deal damage with a melee attack.* |
| --- |

| You channel divine energy through your weapon at the moment of impact. Spend up to 3 Conviction (you cannot spend more than your current maximum Conviction capacity). The weapon hit itself is unchanged and still resolves against the target's Vigor Defense. Judgment then deals a separate instance of Radiant damage: roll one Resolve Die per Conviction spent (maximum 3 dice) and resolve that Radiant damage against the target's Resolve Defense, the Defense matching its Radiant damage type. Because this is a distinct magical instance and not dice pooled into the weapon's Impact Roll, it is not limited by the Bonus Impact Dice cap. |
| --- |

| *Note: Judgment can only be used once per turn.* |
| --- |

| **Aura of Resolve  │  Level 1  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| Your conviction radiates outward, fortifying those who fight alongside you. All allies within 10ft of you Upgrade their Resolve Die for any Resolve Defense Save they make. This aura is always active while you are conscious. At Level 9, the radius extends to 30ft. |
| --- |

| **Martyr's Blood  │  Level 2  │  Reaction  │  Cost: 2 Conviction** |
| --- |

| ***Trigger: ****An ally within 30ft takes damage that would reduce them to 0 Hit Points.* |
| --- |

| As a Reaction, when an ally within 30ft would be reduced to 0 Hit Points by an attack, spend 2 Conviction to intercede. That damage is redirected to you instead: your ally takes no damage from that attack, and you take it in full against your own Defense Ratings. |
| --- |

| *Note: Martyr**'**s Blood combined with high Vigor Die and Heavy Armor makes the redirection survivable in most cases. The additional Conviction generation from taking the hit means sacrifice fuels further sacrifice.* |
| --- |

| **Ability Die Increase  │  Levels 3, 5, 7, and 9  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| Permanently Upgrade one Attribute Die of your choice by one step on the Step-Die scale. Because your Defense Ratings are derived from your Attribute Dice, improving an Attribute Die simultaneously improves the corresponding Defense Rating baseline. |
| --- |

| **Litanies of War  │  Level 4  │  Quick Action  │  Cost: Varies (Conviction)** |
| --- |

| At Level 4, you master three Litanies of War: concentrated expressions of divine will that reshape your capabilities. You may activate one Litany per turn as a Quick Action by spending Conviction. Only one Litany may be active at a time. Activating a new Litany ends the previous one immediately. |
| --- |

| ▪  **Litany of Iron (Cost: 1 to 2 Conviction): **You forge your conviction into an impenetrable shell. For each Conviction spent (maximum 2 for this Litany), all Impact Rolls targeting you this round have their Impact Pool Downgraded once. Spending 2 Conviction Downgrades every die in every incoming Impact Pool twice this round. This applies to all attacks made against you until the start of your next turn. |
| --- |

| ▪  **Litany of Wrath (Cost: 1 to 3 Conviction): **You channel righteous fury into your strikes. For each Conviction spent, roll one additional Vigor Die on your next melee attack and add it to that attack's Impact Roll as physical damage against the same Defense. Spending 3 Conviction adds 3 Vigor Dice of bonus damage to your next single melee attack. These bonus dice are a sanctioned capped exception to the Bonus Impact Dice rule, which normally permits only one bonus die: they are added dice (maximum 3), not Upgrade-stacking. |
| --- |

| ▪  **Litany of Alacrity (Cost: 1 to 3 Conviction): **You move with divine purpose. For each Conviction spent, your Base Speed increases by 10ft until the start of your next turn. Spending 3 Conviction grants 30ft of additional Speed for that duration. Speed is an inherently flat measurement in this system: this Speed increase is a sanctioned flat value. |
| --- |

| **Multiattack  │  Level 5  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| Your divine authority drives relentless martial pressure. You may attack twice instead of once whenever you take the Attack action on your turn. You may use the Combined Strike rule to pool both attacks into a single Impact Roll against the same target, subtracting their Defense Rating only once. |
| --- |

| **Divine Surge  │  Level 5  │  Standard Action  │  Cost: 3 Conviction** |
| --- |

| You release a burst of concentrated radiant energy in a 15ft radius centered on yourself. Roll your Resolve Die against the Resolve Defense of all enemies within the radius. On a hit, each takes Radiant damage equal to your Resolve Die and is Dazed until the end of their next turn. |
| --- |

| **Vitality Surge  │  Levels 5 and 10  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| At Level 5 and again at Level 10, your maximum Hit Points increase by an additional amount equal to the maximum face value of your current Vigor Die, in addition to the standard level-up HP roll. |
| --- |

| **Censure  │  Level 7  │  Standard Action  │  Cost: 2 Conviction** |
| --- |

| You level your gaze at one target within 60ft and speak a word of divine authority that strips away their will to act. Roll your Resolve Die against the target's Resolve Defense. On a hit, the target is Dazed and cannot move toward you on their next turn: they may move laterally or away but your presence repels their advance. On a Glancing Blow, the target cannot move toward you until the end of their next turn but is not Dazed. |
| --- |

| **Unbreakable  │  Level 8  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| Your faith makes you immune to conditions that attack the spirit. You are immune to the Dazed and Blinded conditions. Additionally, you cannot be Grappled by any creature with an equal or lower Vigor Die than your own: they simply cannot overpower you physically. |
| --- |

| **Avatar of War  │  Level 10  │  Standard Action  │  Cost: 5 Conviction  │  Once Per Combat** |
| --- |

| You become a manifestation of divine wrath and protection for one minute. While Avatar of War is active: all three Litanies of War are active simultaneously without requiring activation or Conviction expenditure, Judgment may be used on every hit rather than once per turn, your Aura of Resolve Upgrades allies' Resolve Die twice instead of once, and all Impact Rolls targeting you have their entire Impact Pool Downgraded. When Avatar of War ends, you generate your maximum Conviction (5 points) as the divine energy settles back into you. |
| --- |

**ZEALOT DOCTRINES (SUBCLASSES)**

At Level 3, you define the nature of your divine covenant. Your Doctrine grants features at Levels 3, 6, and 9.

**DOCTRINE OF BLACK IRON**

*The Bastion: You swear an oath to endure. Your armor is your sermon and your survival is your testimony.*

| **Iron Skin  │  Level 3  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| Your conviction literally hardens your flesh beneath your armor. While wearing Heavy Armor, whenever you take Physical damage (Slashing, Piercing, or Bludgeoning), roll your Resolve Die and reduce the final damage by the result after your Vigor Defense has been applied. This reduction represents faith absorbing a portion of each blow after armor handles the rest. |
| --- |

| **Fortress of One  │  Level 6  │  Standard Action  │  Cost: 2 Conviction** |
| --- |

| You plant yourself as an immovable point of divine authority. Spend 2 Conviction to enter Fortress of One. While active: you cannot be moved against your will, cannot be knocked Prone, and all Impact Rolls targeting you are Downgraded. Additionally, any ally within 5ft of you also cannot be knocked Prone or forcibly moved while you maintain this stance. You may end Fortress of One at the start of any of your turns as a Free Action. |
| --- |

| **The Inviolate Form  │  Level 9  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| Your body becomes a testament to divine durability. You are immune to the Exhausted condition. Additionally, whenever you would gain Decay from an Entropy attack, roll your Resolve Die: if the result equals or exceeds your current Decay stage number, the Decay does not advance. Your conviction actively resists the erosion of void energy. |
| --- |

**DOCTRINE OF THE ETERNAL FLAME**

*The Inquisitor: Your faith burns. You specialize in radiant offensive power and the absolute destruction of those who oppose your cause.*

| **Burning Conviction  │  Level 3  │  Passive** |
| --- |

| Your Judgment burns with exceptional intensity. Whenever you use Judgment, the target also suffers the Bleeding condition as the radiant energy sears their wounds open: they take 2 True Damage at the start of each of their turns until treated. |
| --- |

| **Inquisitor's Mark  │  Level 6  │  Quick Action  │  Cost: 1 Conviction** |
| --- |

| You brand one target within 60ft with a mark of divine judgment. The mark is visible as a faintly glowing sigil. While marked: your attacks against the target are Upgraded, the target cannot benefit from Cover against you, and if the target attempts to move more than 30ft away from you, they take Radiant damage equal to your Resolve Die automatically with no roll required. The mark persists until the target is reduced to 0 HP or until you mark a new target. |
| --- |

| **Flame of Judgment  │  Level 9  │  Standard Action  │  Cost: 4 Conviction** |
| --- |

| You call down a pillar of divine fire on one target within 60ft. This is a Visceral strike. Roll your Resolve Die against the target's Focus Defense to connect; on a hit, deal 3 Resolve Dice of Radiant damage as True Damage and the target suffers the Bleeding condition. On a Glancing Blow the strike deals no damage and Bleeding does not apply, but you gain 1 Momentum. |
| --- |

**DOCTRINE OF THE SHEPHERD**

*The Protector: Your faith flows outward. You are not just a warrior; you are the reason your allies survive.*

| **Shepherd's Grace  │  Level 3  │  Quick Action  │  Cost: 1 Conviction** |
| --- |

| When you adopt this Doctrine, you gain spellcasting access to the Resonance and Abjuration schools. Additionally, you channel divine healing into one ally within 30ft: spend 1 Conviction to have that ally roll their Vigor Die and regain that many Hit Points. You may use Shepherd's Grace multiple times per round as long as you have Conviction to spend, but each use costs a Quick Action. Each use must target a different ally: you cannot use it twice on the same ally in the same turn. |
| --- |

| **Sanctuary of Steel  │  Level 6  │  Reaction  │  Cost: 2 Conviction** |
| --- |

| ***Trigger: ****An ally within 15ft is targeted by an attack.* |
| --- |

| As a Reaction, when an ally within 15ft is targeted by an attack, spend 2 Conviction to interpose your divine authority. The attacker's entire Impact Pool is Downgraded for that attack. |
| --- |

| **Divine Restoration  │  Level 9  │  Standard Action  │  Cost: 3 Conviction** |
| --- |

| You lay hands on one adjacent ally and channel a surge of healing energy that repairs both body and spirit. The ally immediately rolls their Vigor Die and regains that many Hit Points. Additionally, remove up to two conditions from the ally: choose from Bleeding, Blinded, Dazed, or Prone. If the ally is suffering from the Decay condition, their Decay retreats by one stage. |
