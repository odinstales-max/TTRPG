CLASS FEATURES


 The Primal Fury │ Level 1 │ Passive




 You turn pain and the heat of battle into Fury, a volatile combat resource. You begin each combat with 0
 Fury and may hold up to your Max Fury (2 at Level 1, rising to 5 by Level 10); it empties when combat
 ends or you fall Incapacitated. The Primal Fury also grants two quick reserve options. Bloodied Resilience
 (Quick Action │ Cost: 1 Vigor Point): roll your Vigor Die and gain that many temporary Hit Points. Defiance
 (Quick Action │ Cost: 1 Resolve Point): Upgrade your next Save before the start of your next turn, and end
 the Frightened or Dazed condition on yourself if you are suffering from it.




 Generating Fury




 ▪ Blood Drawn: The first time each round that an enemy targets you with an attack — melee, ranged, or
 spell — you generate 1 Fury, whether or not it lands. Ongoing damage (Bleeding, Decay), hazards, and
 traps never count: only a direct attack stokes your Fury.
Unarmored Bulwark │ Level 1 │ Passive




Your rage hardens flesh where steel would only slow you. While you are wearing no armor and not
wielding a shield, your Vigor Defense and your Resolve Defense each gain a bonus equal to half the
maximum face value of your Resolve Die (d6: +3, d8: +4, d10: +5, d12: +6). Wearing any armor suppresses
this feature until it is removed. As your will grows, so does the wall: Ability Die Increases spent on Resolve
raise this bonus automatically.




Furious Strike │ Level 1 │ Free Action │ Cost: 1 to 3 Fury




*Trigger: **When you deal damage with a melee attack.*




Your rage adds crushing weight to your blows. When you hit an enemy with a melee attack, you may
spend Fury up to your current total, to a maximum of 3, and add one bonus Vigor Die to that attack's
Impact Roll for each Fury spent (so you can reach the full 3 dice only once your Max Fury allows it, at
Level 4). This is a deliberate exception to the one-bonus-die limit of the Bonus Impact Dice rule, and it
may be combined with the Combined Strike rule. If you spend 3 Fury, the target must succeed on a Vigor
Save (Difficulty 5) or be knocked Prone. Furious Strike scales as your Vigor Die climbs the Step-Die scale,
not by stacking extra dice or Upgrades onto a single blow.




Reckless Attack │ Level 1 │ Free Action │ Cost: None




*Trigger: **At the start of your turn, before taking any actions.*




You abandon all pretense of defense to strike with maximum ferocity. Declare Reckless Attack at the start
of your turn. For the remainder of your turn, one Impact Die of your choice in each melee Impact Roll you
make is Upgraded (one die per Impact Roll, chosen before rolling). Until the start of your next turn, any
enemy that targets you with a melee attack has their Impact Die Upgraded against you as well.
*Note: Reckless Attack is a true trade: more damage dealt, more damage taken. Goading Roar controls
who is doing the attacking while the engine runs.*




Blood Armor │ Level 2 │ Reaction │ Cost: 1 Fury




*Trigger: **When you take damage.*




As a Reaction, when you take damage, spend 1 Fury to flex through the pain. Roll your Vigor Die and
reduce the incoming damage by the result. This reduction is applied before the damage affects your Hit
Points.




Wrathful Stride │ Level 2 │ Quick Action │ Cost: 1 Fury




Your rage carries you across the field like a thrown spear. As a Quick Action, spend 1 Fury to immediately
move up to 15ft in a straight line toward an enemy you can see. This movement ignores difficult terrain
and does not trigger Opportunity Attacks.




Ability Die Increase │ Levels 3, 5, 7, and 9 │ Passive




Permanently Upgrade one Attribute Die of your choice by one step on the Step-Die scale. Because your
Defense Ratings are derived from your Attribute Dice, improving an Attribute Die simultaneously
improves the corresponding Defense Rating baseline. You gain this feature at Levels 3, 5, 7, and 9.




Goading Roar │ Level 4 │ Standard Action │ Cost: 1 Vigor Point




You unleash a challenging roar that shakes the resolve of every nearby enemy. Roll your Resolve Die
against the Resolve Defense of all enemies within 15ft. On a hit, the target is Dazed and, until the end of
your next turn, their Impact Die is Downgraded on any attack they make against a target other than you.
On a Glancing Blow, only the Downgrade against targets other than you applies.
Multiattack │ Level 5 │ Passive




You strike with feral speed. You may attack twice instead of once whenever you take the Attack action on
your turn. You may use the Combined Strike rule to pool both attacks into a single Impact Roll against the
same target, subtracting their Defense Rating only once.




Vitality Surge │ Levels 5 and 10 │ Passive




At Level 5 and again at Level 10, your maximum Hit Points increase by an additional amount equal to the
maximum face value of your current Vigor Die, in addition to the standard level-up HP roll.




Danger Sense │ Level 7 │ Reaction │ Cost: 2 Vigor Points




*Trigger: **You trigger a trap or are caught in an area-of-effect attack.*




As a Reaction, when you trigger a trap or are targeted by an area-of-effect attack, spend 2 Vigor Points to
hurl yourself out of harm's way. You instantly move up to 15ft in any direction and take zero damage from
the triggering effect. This movement does not trigger Opportunity Attacks.




Devastating Leap │ Level 8 │ Standard Action │ Cost: 2 Vigor Points




You cross the battlefield in a single terrifying bound, slamming down with seismic force. Launch yourself
up to 30ft to an unoccupied space. Roll your Vigor Die against the Vigor Defense of all enemies within 10ft
of your landing zone. On a hit, each takes Bludgeoning damage equal to two rolls of your Vigor Die and is
knocked Prone. On a Glancing Blow, each target's Speed is halved until the end of their next turn as the
shockwave staggers them.
 Relentless │ Level 10 │ Passive │ Once Per Combat




 Your fury refuses to let your body die. Once per combat, when you are reduced to 0 Hit Points, you may
 choose to drop to 1 Hit Point instead. If you do, you instantly generate your maximum Fury (5 points) and
 your next attack automatically triggers an Explosion: roll the damage die, add the maximum face value of
 that die to the result, then continue any further Explosion chain normally.




 *Note: Relentless does not prevent death from effects that explicitly bypass the death save system.*



BERSERKER PATHS (SUBCLASSES)
At Level 3, you choose how to direct your rage. Your Path grants features at Levels 3, 6, and 9.

PATH OF BLOODFURY
*The Executioner: You draw power from massive wounds and spilled blood, growing more lethal the more
punishment you absorb.*


 Crimson Tide │ Level 3 │ Passive




 Pain only makes you stronger. While you are at or below half of your maximum Hit Points, whenever you
 roll damage for a melee attack, roll one additional Vigor Die and add it to the total. This bonus die can
 Explode normally.




 Blood for Blood │ Level 6 │ Reaction │ Cost: 2 Fury




 *Trigger: **An enemy within melee reach deals damage to you.*




 As a Reaction, when an enemy in melee reach strikes you, spend 2 Fury to immediately make one melee
 attack against them. This attack is made as part of the Reaction and does not consume your Standard
 Action.
 Crimson Rampage │ Level 9 │ Passive




 You become a whirlwind of violence. Whenever you reduce a creature to 0 Hit Points with a melee attack,
 you may immediately move up to 15ft and make one additional melee attack against a different target as
 a Free Action. This additional attack can itself trigger Crimson Rampage if it reduces that target to 0 Hit
 Points.



PATH OF IRON STORM
*The Colossus: You do not wield two massive weapons because it is practical. You wield them because
nothing else comes close to expressing what you are capable of.*


 Twin Fury │ Level 3 │ Passive




 You may wield two Heavy two-handed weapons simultaneously, defying every conventional
 understanding of what a body can sustain. This satisfies the class feature requirement for dual Heavy
 weapon use stated in the Core Rules. When you choose this Path, you acquire or forge a second Heavy
 weapon of your choice. Wielding two Heavy weapons does not change the Heavy property's pacing: you
 still add the Heavy bonus Impact Die once per turn, on your first attack. Whenever your primary weapon's
 Impact Die triggers an Explosion, your offhand weapon automatically strikes the same target as a separate
 roll. Both weapons apply their Explosion Riders independently. That secondary strike can itself Explode
 normally.




 Sharpened Fury │ Level 6 │ Passive




 Your strikes find the breaking point faster than they should. The Explosion threshold for both weapons
 lowers by 1. A d10 now Explodes on 9 or 10. A d12 Explodes on 11 or 12. A threshold result counts as
 rolling the die's maximum value for all Explosion-triggered effects.




 Cascading Wrath │ Level 9 │ Passive




 Your two weapons become a single engine of devastation. Whenever both your primary and offhand
 weapons trigger Explosions in the same turn, each Explosion's bonus die is Upgraded one step before it is
 rolled, and you generate 1 Fury. This Fury generation occurs once per turn regardless of other generation
 triggers.



PATH OF OUTWARD RAGE
*The War Banner: Your fury is not contained. It radiates outward, galvanizing your allies and breaking the
nerve of your enemies.*


 Infectious Fury │ Level 3 │ Passive




 When you generate Fury from Blood Drawn, the nearest ally within 30ft who can see you gains 1
 Momentum Token. Your rage is visible and contagious. Allies watching you absorb punishment and keep
 fighting are galvanized by it. If multiple allies are equidistant, the ally whose turn comes next in the
 initiative order receives the token.




 War Cry │ Level 6 │ Standard Action │ Cost: 2 Fury




 You unleash a roar that electrifies those fighting alongside you. Choose up to two allies within 30ft who
 can hear you; each may immediately make one melee or ranged attack as a Free Action. This attack uses
 their normal Impact Die and does not consume their Standard Action. Allies who are Incapacitated or
 otherwise unable to act are unaffected.




 Tide of Rage │ Level 9 │ Passive




 The raw violence of your combat presence becomes psychologically destabilizing. Whenever you use
 Furious Strike, every enemy within 10ft of you must make a Resolve Save against Difficulty 5. On a failure,
 their Impact Die is Downgraded until the end of their next turn. This Save is triggered by the Furious Strike
 declaration and resolves before your attack damage is applied.
