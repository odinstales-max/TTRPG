THE SPELL LIST: TRANSMUTATION
[Game Name] │ Standardized Spell Reference
Transmutation is the school of physical and chemical change. Where Conjuration creates matter from nothing,
Transmutation alters what is already there. A Transmuter does not fire bolts of energy; they reach into the
physical laws of the battlefield and rewrite them. Stone flows, bodies harden or weaken, poisons form from
the air, and gravity becomes a negotiation.
Transmutation is the most physically varied school. It is the only school that Upgrades an ally's Attribute Dice
to make them stronger, the only one that reshapes the caster's own body through Shapeshifting, the only one
that wields Petrification, and the only one that permanently reshapes terrain. Its damage is Acid and Poison
against Agility Defense, or Bludgeoning against Vigor Defense, and it corrodes matter and defenses where
Entropy erodes the essence within.
A damage spell rolls its Focus Dice against the named defense; reduced to 0 is a Glancing Blow and you gain 1
Momentum. A spell that weakens or transforms an unwilling target is resisted by a Save, and on a pass you
gain 1 Momentum. Enhancing or reshaping a willing target or yourself is never contested. All Transmutation
spells require a Standard Action unless stated otherwise.
The Petrification Condition. Petrification advances in three stages. At the start of each affected turn the target
makes a Vigor Save against Difficulty 7; a failure advances one stage and a success holds. Stage 1: Speed is
halved and attackers Downgrade Impact Dice that need precision. Stage 2: Speed drops to 0 and the target
loses its Reaction, though it may still act and attack. Stage 3: the target is Restrained and treated as partly
stone, all damage to it is True Damage, attackers Upgrade against it, and all its Defense Ratings except Vigor
Defense drop to 0. Removing stages requires anti-transmutation magic.
