THE SPELL LIST: ENTROPY
[Game Name] │ Standardized Spell Reference
Entropy is the school of void magic, decay, and the dissolution of living systems. It does not conjure, protect,
or deceive. It accelerates the inevitable, corroding enemies until nothing remains to resist.
All Entropy damage is Void and targets Focus Defense. Roll your Focus Dice against the target's Focus Defense
and deal the remainder; reduced to 0 is a Glancing Blow and you gain 1 Momentum. Three things make
Entropy unlike any other school. It is the only school that heals by dealing damage, drawing life force back to
the caster or an ally. It is the only school with Decay, a stacking corruption that progressively Sunders all four
of a target's defenses. And at the highest tiers its Soul Erosion strikes deal True Damage, bypassing defenses
entirely.
Void Drain healing has no effect against undead or constructs, which have no living force to siphon. Soul
Erosion strikes are Visceral: they roll against Focus Defense to connect, but deal their damage as True
Damage. Effects that Downgrade the caster's dice still apply to a Visceral strike, shrinking the roll before it
meets the Defense Rating; effects that reduce incoming damage after a hit do not. All Entropy spells require a
Standard Action unless stated otherwise.
The Decay Condition. Decay is unique to Entropy. The first Entropy hit applies Decay Stage 1; each later
Entropy hit on an already-Decaying target advances it one stage. Decay does not advance on a Glancing Blow.
Stage 1 Sunders Vigor Defense by 1. Stage 2 adds Agility Defense by 1. Stage 3 adds Focus Defense by 1. Stage
4 adds Resolve Defense by 1, leaving the target Sundered across all four defenses. No defense drops below 0.
Decay persists until cleansed by a Long Rest.

Cost: 0 Focus Points. Roll 1 Focus Die when a roll is required. The first Decay, the first drain, the baseline void
burst, and the reactive interrupt.

 Effect: Channel void energy into a target's body. Roll your Focus Die against their Focus Defense and deal
 Void damage equal to the remainder. If the target is not yet Decaying, it enters Decay Stage 1; if it already
 is, its Decay advances one stage.

 you re-mark, or combat ends.

TIER 1 — PRACTICED ENTROPY
Cost: 1 to 2 Focus Points. Roll 1 Focus Die. Sustained Decay, the core life-drain, the scaling void zone, and
direct unraveling of active magic.

 Cost: 1 Focus Point │ Target: Focus Defense │ Range: Touch │ Tags: Decay
 Effect: Drive sustained void energy into a target through contact. Roll your Focus Die against their Focus
 Defense, deal Void damage equal to the remainder, and apply or advance Decay one stage. Your next
 Impact Die against this target is Upgraded as its defenses open.


 Doom Brand                                                                                                   Tier 1

 Effect: Brand a target with an accelerating doom. Roll your Focus Die against their Focus Defense and deal
 Void damage equal to the remainder. While you Channel this spell, the target's Decay advances one stage
 at the start of each of your turns even if no attack strikes it. The Channel breaks if the target falls or you fall
 Unconscious.

 Unweaving                                                                                                      Tier 1

 Soul Severance                                                                                              Tier 3
 Cost: 6 Focus Points │ Target: Focus Defense │ Range: 60ft │ Tags: Soul Erosion
 Effect: Focus the totality of void into one point of absolute erosion. This is Visceral: roll 3 Focus Dice against

 rolls death saves as normal.
