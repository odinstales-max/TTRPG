# THE SPELL LIST: EVOCATION

*[Game Name]  │  Standardized Spell Reference*

Evocation is the school of raw, weaponized magical energy. Where other schools reshape the world or bend minds, Evocation takes magical force and turns it outward with maximum effect. It is the school that does the most direct damage in the game.

Evocation works through five elements, and each carries a fixed signature. Fire leaves targets Burning, dealing damage over time. Frost slows and freezes, stripping movement. Volt Downgrades a target's next attack and chains to a second creature. Force pushes and repositions, and all Force damage ignores Cover. Thunder Sunders defenses with concussive shockwaves. The element you choose is the effect you get.

Nearly every Evocation spell deals damage: roll your Focus Dice against the target's Agility Defense and deal the remainder, unless the spell names a different defense. Reduced to 0 is a Glancing Blow and you gain 1 Momentum. A few spells impose a condition without damage; those are resisted by a Save, and if the target passes you gain 1 Momentum. All Evocation spells require a Standard Action unless stated otherwise.
