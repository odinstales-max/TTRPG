# THE SPELL LIST: ABJURATION

*[Game Name]  │  Standardized Spell Reference*

Abjuration is the school of protection, negation, and denial. Where Evocation turns magical energy outward as a weapon, Abjuration turns it inward as a shield, then turns that shield against those who strike at the people it guards.

Abjuration is the dedicated anti-magic school. Other schools can interfere with enemy magic in a single narrow way, but Abjuration alone counters spells as they are cast, suppresses casting across whole zones, strips active enchantments, and reflects hostile magic at its source. Its protections are equally varied: warding away blows, granting cover, absorbing damage, and bonding casters to the allies they shield.

Abjuration deals little damage, and when it does it is Force or Radiant, the energy of warding made violent. Protection, healing, and cleansing are never contested against a willing target, so they need no roll. When Abjuration denies an enemy something, that enemy resists with a Save, and if they pass you gain 1 Momentum. The rare damage spell rolls against the named defense, and a hit reduced to 0 gives you 1 Momentum. All Abjuration spells require a Standard Action unless stated otherwise.
