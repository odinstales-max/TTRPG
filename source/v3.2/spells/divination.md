# THE SPELL LIST: DIVINATION

*[Game Name]  │  Standardized Spell Reference*

Divination is the school of information, awareness, and foresight. It does not reshape matter, raise barriers, or summon creatures. It reshapes what the party knows, turning the battlefield's hidden structure into a systematic advantage that compounds every round. A Diviner's power is invisible until it is decisive.

Divination's only damage type is Psychic, which targets Resolve Defense. Beyond a few Psychic strikes, Divination wins fights by knowing more, acting sooner, and denying enemies the information they need to respond.

A damage spell rolls its Focus Dice and subtracts the target's Resolve Defense; reduced to 0 is a Glancing Blow and you gain 1 Momentum. A hostile spell that imposes a condition is resisted by a Save the target rolls against the listed Difficulty; if they pass, you gain 1 Momentum. Marking and revelation simply take effect. All Divination spells require a Standard Action unless stated otherwise.
