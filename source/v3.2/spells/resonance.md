# THE SPELL LIST: RESONANCE

*[Game Name]  │  Standardized Spell Reference*

Resonance is the school of the mind turned weapon. It does not create fire or conjure stone. It reaches into what is genuinely present inside a creature and pulls: the rage already burning becomes a wildfire, the doubt already flickering becomes paralysis, the loyalty already fraying becomes betrayal. A Resonance caster does not deceive the senses or rewrite reality; they find what is already there and make it catastrophic.

This is the school of compelled actions and stolen decisions, of connections forged between minds under fire and stretched into conduits for devastation. It is the third school of the mind, and it is defined against the other two: where Divination reveals true information and Illusion fabricates false perception, Resonance seizes volition itself. It alone forces and replaces a target's actions, it alone forms Resonant Links, and it alone turns emotion into a weapon.

All Resonance damage is Psychic and targets Resolve Defense. A damage spell rolls its Focus Dice against Resolve Defense; reduced to 0 is a Glancing Blow and you gain 1 Momentum. A spell that compels or dominates is resisted by a Save against the listed Difficulty, and on a pass you gain 1 Momentum. Inspiring or bonding an ally is never contested. No Resonance effect ever removes a target's whole turn; compulsion redirects an action, and domination replaces one, but the target always keeps their movement and survival instinct. All Resonance spells require a Standard Action unless stated otherwise.

Resonant Links, Compulsion, and Domination. A Resonant Link forms when two or more creatures take Psychic damage from the same Resonance spell in the same round, or when a spell states it establishes a Link. Links persist until a linked creature reaches 0 HP or combat ends, and spells that exploit Links grow stronger as Links accumulate. Compulsion forces an action on a target's own turn and never eliminates the turn. Domination replaces one of a target's actions with a command you insert, but the target keeps their movement, retains survival instinct, and will not knowingly walk into certain death.
