# THE SPELL LIST: ILLUSION

*[Game Name]  │  Standardized Spell Reference*

Illusion is the school of manufactured perception. It does not conjure matter, redirect force, or alter flesh. It changes what creatures believe they are experiencing, and those false beliefs carry real mechanical weight. A master of Illusion does not fight the enemy; they fight the enemy's senses and their certainty about what is real.

Illusion deals damage only through Phantasm, where a target's own belief in a false horror becomes genuine psychic shock. That damage is Psychic and targets Resolve Defense. Every other effect creates, conceals, or misdirects rather than harming. This is the deliberate opposite of Divination: where a Diviner reveals what is true and assaults the mind directly, an Illusionist fabricates what is false and lets the target wound themselves on it.

A damage spell rolls its Focus Dice against the target's Resolve Defense; reduced to 0 is a Glancing Blow and you gain 1 Momentum. A spell that imposes a condition is resisted by a Save against the listed Difficulty, and on a pass you gain 1 Momentum. Pure fabrications and concealments are not contested by a roll; instead they are governed by disbelief. All Illusion spells require a Standard Action unless stated otherwise.

Belief and Disbelief. Illusions are experienced as real until a creature has concrete cause to doubt them. Three things trigger a chance to disbelieve: physically passing through an illusion, taking zero damage from an attack by an illusory entity, or spending a Quick Action to scrutinize it and succeeding on a Focus Save against the caster's Focus Defense. A creature that disbelieves sees through the illusion entirely and may spend a Quick Action to warn one ally, granting that ally an immediate scrutinize attempt. Sight-based illusions affect only vision; Tier 2 and higher fabrications and concealments affect all senses unless stated otherwise.
