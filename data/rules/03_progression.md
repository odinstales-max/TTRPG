# Progression

There are no classes in [GAME NAME]. What you were given matters less than what you choose to become: every character grows along the same spine of levels, and every meaningful choice is yours. Your capability lives in your four Attribute Dice; your identity lives in the Talents and Traits you take.

## Leveling and Milestones

Characters advance through ten levels. Progression is milestone-based: the GM determines when the party levels up based on story progress, significant accomplishments, and the natural arc of the campaign. There is no experience point tracking. A level-up happens when it feels earned.

The GM should consider leveling the party together. Individual advancement creates mechanical imbalance and can undermine group cohesion. Unless a campaign specifically calls for it, all players level up at the same time.

Level 1 is not on this table. A starting character is built with Creation Points rather than
levelled into: see [Character Creation](02_character_creation.md).

## What Each Level Grants

Every level after 1st grants exactly one of two things, on a strict alternating rhythm:

| Level | Grant |
| --- | --- |
| 2 | Talent |
| 3 | Ability Die Increase |
| 4 | Talent |
| 5 | Ability Die Increase · Vitality Surge · additional Trait `(tunable)` |
| 6 | Talent |
| 7 | Ability Die Increase |
| 8 | Talent |
| 9 | Ability Die Increase |
| 10 | Talent · Vitality Surge · additional Trait `(tunable)` |

Every level after 1st also increases your maximum Hit Points — see [Health & Recovery](13_health_and_recovery.md).

## Ability Die Increases

At Levels 3, 5, 7, and 9, you gain an Ability Die Increase. Permanently Upgrade one Attribute Die of your choice by one step on the Step-Die scale. Because your Defense Ratings are derived from your Attribute Dice, improving an Attribute Die simultaneously improves your offensive capability and the corresponding Defense Rating.

**Natural progression caps at d10.** An Ability Die Increase cannot raise an Attribute Die above d10. The four increases at 3, 5, 7, and 9 are the only leveling path upward, and d10 is where that path ends.

## Reaching d12

A permanent d12 Attribute is Legendary for a reason: leveling alone never gets you there. Only an Upgrade granted beyond the natural cap can, and there are exactly two sources:

- **The capstone Talent.** A Level 10 Talent, available only if the chosen Attribute is already at d10, permanently Upgrades that one Attribute to d12. (Defined in the Talent list, Phase 2.)
- **Items and boons.** Rare artifacts and story boons may grant the d10→d12 Upgrade at the GM's discretion. These are exceptional rewards, not build components.

Temporary roll Upgrades (from cover, assists, Talents, and similar effects) can still push a d10 roll to d12 and beyond into overflow, exactly as described in [Upgrading and Downgrading Rolls](01_attributes_and_dice.md). The cap applies to the die you own, not the roll you make.

## Talents and Traits

Talents (gained at Levels 2, 4, 6, 8, and 10) and Traits (chosen at creation, with more at Levels 5 and 10 `(tunable)`) are the whole of character identity in v4. Their rules live in [Talents & Traits](04_talents_and_traits.md).
