# Momentum

Momentum Tokens represent a character accumulating energy, focus, and resolve through the friction of active play. The eleven old resource pools are gone — Fury, Guile, Velocity, Animus, Conviction, Lethality, Acclaim, Aether, Clarity, Accord, Entropy — collapsed into this one. The flavor of how *your* character builds and spends momentum lives in your Talent text, not in the name of the pool.

Momentum is an intentional exception to the system's preference for dice-based expression of power: momentum is small, concrete, and accumulates through persistence rather than fortune.

## The Pool

- **One pool per character.** Each character maintains their own personal pool of Momentum Tokens. Tokens do not belong to the group and cannot be shared passively.
- **Pool Cap:** each character may hold a maximum of **3** Momentum Tokens at any time. Talents may raise this cap. Tokens generated beyond your cap are lost.
- **Pool Reset:** all Momentum Tokens reset to 0 at the **start of each combat**. Every fight is built from nothing — momentum is the escalating rhythm of the fight you are in, not a stockpile carried between them. Tokens generated outside combat (from failed Tasks and similar triggers) persist and may be spent on Tasks, but they clear the moment the next combat begins.

## Generating Momentum

Momentum is generated by Talents, GM discretion, and the following universal triggers:

- A melee or ranged attack that results in a **Glancing Blow** generates 1 token for the attacker.
- A spell or ability resisted by a successful **Save** generates 1 token for the caster.
- A Task roll that **fails by 1 or 2** generates 1 token for the rolling character.
- A Task roll that **fails by 3 or more** generates 2 tokens for the rolling character.

Failure is never just loss; it is momentum waiting to be turned.

**Talent generators** add further triggers — "gain 1 Momentum when an enemy attacks you," and the like — all feeding this same pool. There are no separate trackers. Throughout the spell lists and Talent entries, the phrase "you gain 1 Momentum" means that specific character gains 1 token in their personal pool, subject to their cap.

## Spending Momentum

There are exactly two ways to spend a token:

- **Flat spend (shareable):** spend 1 or more of your own tokens before a roll. Each token adds +1 to the final total. You may spend on any roll you make, or on any roll made by an ally within your immediate engagement — a deliberate act of support, not an automatic resource transfer. Multiple tokens may be spent on a single roll. Tokens must be committed before the dice are rolled. This works on Impact Rolls in combat and Task rolls outside it.

- **Talent spend (self-only):** Talents define effects purchased with Momentum. These are **always self-only** and never shareable — your Talents channel your momentum, no one else's.
