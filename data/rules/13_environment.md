# Environment

## Range

Melee reach is 5 feet for all characters unless modified by size, a weapon property, or a Talent. Range for all other attacks, spells, and abilities is defined in the entry for that weapon, spell, or ability. There are no universal range increment penalties in this system; each source specifies its own effective range and any rules that apply beyond it.

## Light and Darkness

Visibility operates on two states: lit and dark. Any light source, regardless of size, fully negates darkness within its radius as defined by that source. There is no intermediate dim light state.

In darkness, all sight-based Task and Save rolls are Downgraded. Impact Rolls made against targets in darkness are also Downgraded. These penalties apply whether you are in darkness yourself or your target is. A character who cannot see their target suffers the full penalty regardless of their own lighting conditions.

Darkvision, the ability to see normally in darkness, is rare. It is not a standard trait of any origin and is only available through specific Talents, spells, or items. When a character with Darkvision is in darkness, they ignore all darkness penalties entirely.

## Falling Damage

Falls of 10 feet or less deal no damage. For every 5 feet fallen beyond the initial 10, the falling creature takes 1d4 True Damage. This damage bypasses all Defense Ratings. A creature falling 20 feet takes 2d4 True Damage. A creature falling 30 feet takes 4d4 True Damage, and so on. There is no maximum cap; falling from great heights is lethal.

Falling damage dice do not explode. A creature that is already Incapacitated when it falls takes the damage normally and the result is applied to their death track immediately.
