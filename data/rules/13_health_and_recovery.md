# Health & Recovery

## Health Tiers

Your Health Tier sets your starting Hit Points and is bought at character creation, where a
tougher floor costs Creation Points you cannot then spend on dice, access, or Specialties.
The full cost table lives in [Character Creation](02_character_creation.md).

| **Health Tier** | **Starting HP** | **Creation cost** | **Built For** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bulwark | 11 + Vigor Die maximum | 10 CP | Immovable defenders who exist to be hit |
| Frontline | 9 + Vigor Die maximum | 6 CP | Holding the line and absorbing punishment |
| Skirmisher | 7 + Vigor Die maximum | 3 CP | Mobile fighters who pick their moments |
| Adept | 5 + Vigor Die maximum | 0 CP | Those who solve problems before they reach melee |

Everything after level 1 is identical for every tier: you buy your floor, not your rate of
growth.

## Gaining Hit Points

At every level after 1st, you roll your Vigor Die and add your character level to the result to increase your maximum HP. This roll cannot explode. If the result is less than half the maximum face value of your Vigor Die, it is rounded up to that half value.

### Vitality Surge

At Level 5 and again at Level 10, every character undergoes a Vitality Surge. In addition to the standard Vigor Die roll, your maximum HP increases by an additional amount equal to the maximum face value of your current Vigor Die.

## Death and Dying

When reduced to 0 Hit Points, you fall Incapacitated. At the start of each of your turns, make a Vigor Save against Difficulty 5:

- **Stable:** Three successes: you stabilize. You remain unconscious but are no longer dying.

- **Death:** Three failures: you die.

- **Defying Death:** Your Vigor Save rolls its maximum face value and Explodes: you instantly regain 1 Hit Point and stand up.

## Healing

When a character receives healing, they regain Hit Points equal to the amount specified by the source. Healing cannot raise a character above their maximum Hit Points. Excess healing is lost.

A character at 0 Hit Points who receives any healing immediately regains that many Hit Points and is no longer Incapacitated. They stand up, their death track resets, and they may act normally on their next turn. Healing does not require the character to have stabilized first. Any healing applied to an Incapacitated character brings them back.

## Rest and Recovery

### Short Rest

Requires 30 minutes of relative calm. Roll your Vigor Die and regain that many Hit Points. Can be done any number of times provided a Healer's Kit or bandages are available. Does not restore Daily Reserves.

### Long Rest

Requires 6 hours of sleep and 2 hours of light activity. Restores all lost Hit Points and all spent Daily Reserves.
