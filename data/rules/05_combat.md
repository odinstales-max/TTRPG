# Combat & Impact

## Impact Dice

Combat in [GAME NAME] does not use to-hit rolls. You always land your strikes. The question is never whether you connect, it is whether the force behind your blow is sufficient to penetrate what stands between you and your target's body.

> **CALCULATING DAMAGE: THREE STEPS**
> 1. Roll your Impact Pool. This is the Attribute Die associated with your attack (Vigor for melee, Agility for ranged, Focus for spells, Resolve for authority-based abilities; weapons with the Finesse property may use Agility for melee). Spell Tier and Talents determine how many dice you roll.
> 2. Add the dice together.
> 3. Subtract the appropriate Defense Rating. The remainder is the Hit Points the target loses.
>
> Bonus Impact Dice: unless a feature explicitly states otherwise, a single Impact Roll can include a maximum of one bonus Impact Die granted by Talents or resource expenditure, in addition to the dice of the attack or spell itself. When a feature allows spending multiple resource points on bonus dice, each point beyond the first Upgrades that bonus die by one step rather than adding another die.

## Basic and Featured Attacks

- **Basic Attack (1 AP):** roll 1 Attribute Die — Vigor for melee, Agility for ranged or Finesse melee. No resource cost. This is the attack everyone always has.

- **Featured Attack (1 AP + resources):** an attack granted by a Talent or spell. Alongside its 1 AP, it costs the **Reserve** and/or **Momentum** stated in its entry. That cost buys additional Impact Dice and/or an added effect. Since your Reserve pools are level-limited, combat becomes a resource-management layer sitting on top of the flat 3-AP turn: AP governs *how many* things you do, resource spend governs *how strong* those things are.

## Making Multiple Attacks

There is no baseline multiattack feature in v4, and none is needed: any character may spend 2 or even 3 of their AP on separate attacks. Two Talents extend this further (defined in the Talent list, Phase 2):

- A **Reserve-discount Talent** reduces the Reserve cost of a second attack-adjacent ability used in the same turn `(tunable — Open Item 6)`.
- A **Combined Strike Talent** lets you pool two attacks against a single target into one Impact Roll, subtracting the Defense Rating only once.

## Glancing Blows

If your total Impact is equal to or lower than the target's Defense Rating, you deal zero damage. This is a Glancing Blow. Physical contact was made, but the impact lacked sufficient force to breach the target's defenses. Some Talents and weapons trigger specifically on Glancing Blows. When your attack against an enemy results in a Glancing Blow, you gain 1 Momentum Token. This is automatic, not optional: no committed strike is ever wasted, and a blow that fails to breach a defense still feeds your momentum.

## Explosions and Cascades

If any die in your Impact Pool rolls its maximum value, that die explodes. Roll that die again and add the result to your damage total. An attack can explode infinitely as long as each bonus roll continues to hit the die's maximum value.

### Vicious Cascades

If a weapon or ability possesses the Vicious property, its explosions cascade upward. When a Vicious die explodes, the bonus die you roll is Upgraded by one step (d6 becomes d8, d8 becomes d10, to a maximum of d12). If that upgraded die also rolls its maximum value, it explodes and upgrades again.
