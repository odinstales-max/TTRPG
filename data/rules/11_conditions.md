# Standardized Conditions

The following conditions have consistent mechanical definitions throughout all of [GAME NAME]. When an ability applies a condition, it always means exactly what is written here unless the source explicitly states otherwise.

## Condition Stacking Rules

Conditions from different sources stack freely in terms of their simultaneous effects. A creature can be Restrained, Dazed, and Blinded at the same time with no cap on how many distinct conditions apply. However, identical conditions from multiple sources do not compound. If two sources both apply the Dazed condition, the target is Dazed once; the second application is redundant, not additive. The one explicit exception is Sundered, which stacks deliberately: each application reduces the specified Defense Rating by 1 further, with a floor of 0. All other conditions are binary, present or absent.

- **Dazed:** Cannot take Reactions. The Impact Die for their next attack is Downgraded.

- **Sundered:** One specific Defense Rating (Vigor, Agility, Focus, or Resolve Defense) is reduced by 1. The inflicting ability specifies which. When stated as lasting until the end of the inflictor's next turn, the rating is restored at that time. When stated as permanent, the reduction persists for the remainder of combat. No Defense Rating can be reduced below 0.

- **Bleeding:** Takes 2 True Damage at the start of each of their turns until they or an ally spends 1 AP to treat the wound, or until they receive magical healing. Bleeding never stacks as separate instances; Talents may escalate an existing Bleed by stated flat amounts, to a maximum of 6 True Damage per turn.

- **Restrained:** Speed is reduced to 0. All Impact Rolls made against this target are Upgraded. The Restrained condition does not prevent teleportation or planar movement, a Restrained creature may still use abilities that move them instantaneously if those abilities do not require spending movement. Effects that specifically prevent teleportation must state so explicitly.

- **Prone:** The creature has been knocked to the ground. A Prone creature's Speed is 0 until they spend 1 AP to stand. Impact Rolls made against a Prone creature from within 15ft are Upgraded. Impact Rolls made against a Prone creature from more than 15ft away are Downgraded. A Prone creature may still take actions while on the ground.

- **Exhausted:** All four Attribute Dice are Downgraded.

- **Incapacitated:** Cannot take any actions, move, or speak. Considered an object for targeting purposes.

- **Invisible:** Cannot be seen by ordinary means. Impact Rolls against this target are Downgraded. The Invisible character's first attack before detection is Upgraded.

- **Hidden:** The creature's location is unknown to its enemies. A Hidden creature cannot be targeted by single-target attacks; area-of-effect attacks affect it normally. Attacking, casting a spell, or otherwise revealing yourself ends the Hidden status unless a feature states otherwise. An enemy may spend 1 AP and succeed on a Focus Task against Difficulty 5 to locate a Hidden creature; if they do, the creature is no longer Hidden from that enemy, but Impact Rolls that enemy makes against it are Downgraded until the creature is fully revealed. Becoming Hidden requires a Talent or a position that fully breaks line of sight.

- **Grappled:** The creature is physically held. Its Speed is 0 and it cannot benefit from any effect that would increase its Speed. The Grappled creature may still take actions, but the grappling effect may impose further restrictions as stated by its source. Unless the source states otherwise, a Grappled creature may spend 1 AP to attempt a Vigor Save against Difficulty 5 to break free, and the condition ends if the grappler is Incapacitated or moves out of reach.

- **Blinded:** Cannot see. All Impact Rolls they make are Downgraded. Cannot benefit from Cover.

- **Silenced:** Cannot speak or produce sound. Cannot cast spells with a Verbal component. All Resolve-based Tasks involving speech or persuasion automatically fail.

- **Slowed:** Base Speed is reduced by a stated amount (a fixed number of feet). A Slowed creature cannot benefit from any effect that would increase its Speed while Slowed. Unlike Restrained, a Slowed creature can still move; it simply moves less.

- **Frightened:** The creature Downgrades all Impact Dice on attacks made against the source of its fear, and it cannot willingly move closer to that source. It may otherwise act normally.

- **Concealed:** The creature is partially hidden from sight or sense. Impact Rolls against a Concealed creature are Downgraded. A fully Concealed creature with no available line of sight cannot be targeted by single-target attacks, identical to full Cover, though Concealment may come from sources other than a solid object.

- **Marked:** The creature has been designated by a single source. Allies of the source gain a stated benefit when attacking the Marked creature, defined by the marking ability. A creature can carry only one mark per source at a time; a new mark from the same source replaces the previous one. Marking is a designation, not a contest, and is not resisted by a Save.

- **Burning:** Takes a stated amount of Fire damage at the start of each of its turns for a stated duration, or until the creature or an ally spends 1 AP to extinguish it, or it receives any amount of magical healing. Burning from a single source does not stack with itself; reapplying it refreshes the duration.

## The Decay Condition (Entropy School)

Decay is a stacking condition unique to the Entropy school of magic. It represents the progressive deterioration of a target's physical and magical defenses as void energy corrodes their structure. Decay has four stages. Each time an Entropy attack successfully hits an already-Decaying target, Decay advances by one stage. Decay does not advance on a Glancing Blow. Certain Entropy spells and abilities may advance Decay by additional stages or at accelerated rates as specified in their descriptions.

- **Decay Stage 1:** The target's Vigor Defense is Sundered by 1.

- **Decay Stage 2:** The target's Vigor Defense and Agility Defense are each Sundered by 1 additionally.

- **Decay Stage 3:** The target's Vigor Defense, Agility Defense, and Focus Defense are each Sundered by 1 additionally.

- **Decay Stage 4:** All four of the target's Defense Ratings are each Sundered by 1 additionally.

These Sunder values are cumulative across all four stages. A target at Stage 4 has their Vigor Defense Sundered by 4 total, Agility Defense Sundered by 3 total, Focus Defense Sundered by 2 total, and Resolve Defense Sundered by 1 total, all subject to the standard floor of 0. Decay is a lingering corruption. It persists until cleansed by a Long Rest. It does not expire at the end of combat.

## The Petrification Condition (Transmutation School)

Petrification is a staged condition unique to the Transmutation school, representing a body slowly turning to inert stone. It advances in three stages. At the start of each affected turn, the target makes a Vigor Save against Difficulty 7; a failure advances Petrification one stage and a success holds it at its current stage. Petrification is an escalating hazard the target can always act against, never an instant disable.

- **Petrification Stage 1:** The target's Base Speed is halved, and Impact Dice that rely on precision against it are Downgraded.

- **Petrification Stage 2:** The target's Speed drops to 0 and it loses its Reaction. It may still spend its AP and attack normally, but it cannot move.

- **Petrification Stage 3:** The target is Restrained and treated as partly stone. All damage dealt to it becomes True Damage, Impact Rolls against it are Upgraded, and all of its Defense Ratings except Vigor Defense drop to 0. The target may still take actions, but it cannot move. Removing stages of Petrification requires anti-transmutation magic.

## Resonant Links, Compulsion, and Domination (Resonance School)

These three mechanics are unique to the Resonance school and are referenced throughout its spells.

A **Resonant Link** forms when two or more creatures take Psychic damage from the same Resonance spell in the same round, or when a spell explicitly establishes one. Linked creatures remain connected until one of them reaches 0 Hit Points or combat ends. Spells that exploit Links grow stronger as more Links accumulate on the battlefield.

**Compulsion** forces a specific action onto a target on the target's own turn. A compelled target performs the forced action and then acts freely with the remainder of its turn. Compulsion never removes a target's turn; it directs one part of it.

**Domination** replaces one of a target's actions with a command inserted by the caster. A dominated target keeps its own movement, retains emotional response and basic survival instinct, and will never knowingly walk into certain death. As with Compulsion, Domination directs a target rather than disabling it. No Resonance effect ever removes a creature's whole turn.
