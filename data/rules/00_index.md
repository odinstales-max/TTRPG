# Core Rules v4 — Reading Order

In [GAME NAME], survival is a calculation of risk, momentum, and raw capability. The world is dangerous, and the people who endure it are defined not by what they were given but by what they are willing to push themselves to become. These rules govern how you navigate the land, engage in lethal combat, and endure the hardships the world throws at you. Every mechanic in this chapter is designed around a single principle: your dice tell the story. There are few static modifiers. The die you carry is your capability, and pushing it to its limit is the most dangerous, most exhilarating thing you will ever do.
